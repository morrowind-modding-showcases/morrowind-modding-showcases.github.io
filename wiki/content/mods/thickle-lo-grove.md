Discover a little secret of the Ashlander traders, who live near Iveri Llothri, the unlucky witch... This mod adds an interesting place to visit in the Grazelands, to the east of Zainab camp and to the south of Vos. Made for the Summer Modjam 2022.

## Features:
- A new area to explore.
- Ashlander traders, who have some personal problems and unique OAAB items in-stock.
- A new house for Iveri Llothri or for YOU! You can live there too, notwithstanding the outcome of “Widowmaker” quest (I’ve made all the containers in the house to have large capacity for convenience). 
- Three cute guars (they’re the pets of these Ashlander traders).
- Two cute Silt Striders (one of them is a baby Strider :з ).
- If you’re bored, go show the note from the witch’s hut to the Ashlanders~
- No landscape edits. No “Widowmaker” edits (only slightly tweaked Iveri Llothri’s position).

## World Edits:

Small landscape overhaul mod for a tiny portion of the Grazelands, impacting a small number of exterior cells with new flora, terrain, and clutter assets. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other Grazelands mods, including virtually all other Grazelands landscape overhauls and new locations.
