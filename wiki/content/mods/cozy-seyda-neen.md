A small and modest replacement of Seyda Neen turning it into a cozy fishing village in the Dunmer style.

This mod adds various default objects such as: statics, containers, plants, trees, misc, etc.
Added three new huts, two of which are decorations and locked, and do not contain interiors but perform a decorative function only.

## World Edits:
Primarily adds new clutter and statics to the cells of Seyda Neen, along with three new buildings with new interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits to vanilla cells.

## Other Notes:

This mod was merged into [Lovely Bitter Coast](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49488).

Compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Treees and any and all Seyda Neen interior overhauls. Will likely conflict with any other exterior Seyda Neen overhauls though.
