Adds a cozy new Hlaalu dock town at the mouth of the Odai River, as well as a few quests within the town. West of Seyda Neen, south of Balmora. Also marbles!

## Features:

- Adds a new coastal town with a few shops, an inn, and a quiet dock.
- Adds 3 new quests regarding the secrets of multiple townspeople.
- Practice your alchemy with plenty of herbal ingredients and teas.
- Behold the wonder and glory of the Mudcrab Statue.
- Someone's lost their marbles! Can you find them all?
- Play matchmaker. Or not!
- NEW IN 1.5: Hla Odai travel services, and a related quest!

## World Edits:

Modifies the exterior cells at the base of the Odai River, including landscape edits to the following:

- -5, -7
- -6, -6
- -6, -7
- -6, -8
- -7, -7

## Other Notes:

A compatibility patch is available for Odai - Lifeblood of Balmora. Also, a [compatibility patch is available for The Sable Dragon](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59143).

Confirmed Compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, The Dark Shard, The Imperial Dwemer Society, and OAAB Brother Junipers Twin Lamps.
