> The Manifold Spires house a Temple-affiliated educational institution where mathematics, science, and magic are entwined. No funding for the humanities departments, unfortunately.
>
> Dunmer of the Almsivi faith with great potential for genius are taken here at a young age, where they hone their magical capabilities and intelligence.
>
> One day, the Temple loses contact with this outpost. Nobody can teleport there, and no ships that were sent have come back. What is happening? Perhaps only you, the player, can brave the challenges set forth.
>
> To start this quest, ask around Vivec for the latest rumors.

## Features:

- A completely new landmass in the middle of the ocean with gorgeous visuals.
- A lore-friendly new questline that references the 36 Lessons of Vivec.
- New areas to explore, featuring details in every nook and cranny.

## World Edits

Adds a new landmass to the far east of Vvardenfell, near the Tamriel Rebuilt mainland's eastern coast.

## Other Notes

Reportedly compatible with Tamriel Rebuilt, but has a conflict with the moved TR-compatibility version of Legend of Chemua.
