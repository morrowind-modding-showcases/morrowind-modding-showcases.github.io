Attractive Ascadian Agriculture! Adds polish and detail to the homes, farms, manors and surrounds of the Ascadian Isles, with a "vanilla+" approach, without drastically changing the landscape or aesthetics for maximum modular compatibility.

## World Edits:
Adds landscape detailing, clutter, and OAAB/Tamriel Data asset integration to the following locations in the Ascadian Isles:

- Alof's Farmhouse
- Arano Plantation
- Balur's Farmhouse
- Dirara's Farmhouse
- Gro-Bagrat Plantation
- Llovyn's Farmhouse
- Manat's Farmhouse
- Nilera's Farmhouse
- Omani Manor
- Piernette's Farmhouse
- Small Farmhouse
- Sterdecan's Farmhouse
- Ules Manor (+ Outbuildings)

The interiors and immediate exterior cells around these locations (primarily farm fields, short paths between and leading into farms, manor/plantation yards, docks, and some waterfronts).

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Concept Art Plantations, Ascadian Corkbulb Groves, Bountiful Blue Kanet (minor clipping), and Suran - Pearl of the Ascadian Isles.

Patches are available for Minor Plantations Redone and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.

Will conflict with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage.
