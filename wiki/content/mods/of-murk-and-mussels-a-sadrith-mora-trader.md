This mod adds an unique and detailed salvage shop to Sadrith Mora with a quest and lots of unique clothing and armor assets. BCOM compatible. Now you have at least three reasons to check this mod out!

## Features:
-Adds a shop near the Sadrith Mora market with a beautiful exterior and interior.
-Unique clothing and armor assets such as the functional "glowing" (!) anglerfish helmets and cephalopod armor, mussel amulet ...
-A quest and a detailed grotto to kill your boredom 

## World Edits:

Adds a new shop with associated clutter and new interior to Sadrith Mora. Also adds a new dungeon near Sadrith Mora with some exterior clutter assets.

Adds a handful of quest-related objects/NPCs to other cells.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, OAAB Market, and Sadrith Mora - Seat of Power.

Note: Mod has been merged into Sadrith Mora BCOM Plus Module.
