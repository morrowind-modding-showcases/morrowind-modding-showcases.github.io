> Dubdilla Remade completely rebuilds the cave named Dubdilla from scratch and greatly expands it. Delve into the unfathomable depths of Dubdilla and try to find the odd couple that uses the cave as a hideout from a vengeful father.

## Features

- A completely revamped dungeon experience, turning the cave of Dubdilla into a much more menacing and intricate environment
- A new unique Artifact helmet made by Melchior Dahrk
- loot loot loot
- Tons of verticality
- A tiny bit of new writing

## World Edits

Overhauls the dungeon of Dubdilla. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Compatible with all other mods that don't touch Dubdilla.
