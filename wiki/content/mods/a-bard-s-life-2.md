> Be a bard! Play 40 different songs, sing you enemies to death, make a living singing, open an inn, travel with a companion...

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Ascadian Isles, with landscape edits, as well as a number of quest-related objects and NPCs to a variety of other exterior and interior cells.

## Other Notes

Will likely have some conflicts with mods placing buildings in the same location, and/or editing the landscape around Lake Amaya.
