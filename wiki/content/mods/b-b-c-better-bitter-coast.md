This mod overhauls the landscape and adds denser flora.
Currently, it only covers the area around Seyda Neen.

## World Edits:

Very heavy clutter and detail overhaul of the landscape around Seyda Neen, adding new flora, terrain, building, and static clutter, including landscape edits.

Makes NO edits to interior cells.

## Other Notes:

Mod is still in active development and subject to change in the future. Please update this page with new versions when they come out.

Confirmed minor conflicts with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees and Little Landscapes - Bitter Coast Waterway. Confirmed compatible with A Hermit's Request and The Plague Doctor.

Otherwise, will conflict with nearly all other mods in the same area, including Shipyards of Vvardenfell.
