> The character creation questions for The Elder Scrolls I: Arena referenced a deity who has never been seen since then. Until now.
> Can you stop a new threat from taking hold on Vvardenfell?
>
> To begin the quest, walk the streets of Ald'ruhn after reaching at least level 10.
> (Make sure you're strong enough to wander around Red Mountain, and have some fire resistance.)

## World Edits

Minor quest-related additions to a variety of exterior cells around Red Mountain. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most other mods, no reported conflicts but may have issues with mods that drastically change the shape and size of Red Mountain.
