The fourth installment in my Epic Cities mods, it expands the presence of Dwemer ruins surrounding the village to play up Dagon Fel's unique attractions and charm.

## World Edits:

Primarily an exterior overhaul and expansion of Dagon Fel, adding new structures, clutter and detail to the environs of Dagon Fel, as well as making landscape edits.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Largely incompatible with other Dagon Fel overhauls. Elements of this mod have since been incorporated into other mods.
