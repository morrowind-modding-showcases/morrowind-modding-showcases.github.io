> Vivec Silt Strider Port is an upgrade to the port area and a simple schedule for the driver.
> He will now retire to his new hut for food, to sleep and to get out of bad weather.

## World Edits

Adds new structures and clutter to the exterior cell in front of the Vivec Foreign Quarter Canton, including landscape edits. Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with other silt strider port mods, and may have landscape conflicts with other Vivec mods.
