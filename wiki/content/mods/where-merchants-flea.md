> An orc you might recognize in Suran has had a stroke of bad luck. Bula gra-Muk purchased a pillow from some traveling khajiit. But the pillow came with more than just a fancy cover and she is looking more tired than usual...

## Features:

- Embark on a short errand for Bula who is looking to recover her money
- Experience a random quest path each play through - your target can be in multiple locations!
- Travel all across Vvardenfell (at Bula's expense) on a wild goose chase
- Come into possession of a flea-ridden pillow that might just affect your sleep
- Get a unique tapestry sample to decorate your house ([Perfect Placement](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/46562)﻿ recommended!)
- Get fleas
- Fleas are a source of the jump spell effect

## World Edits

Adds quest NPCs to a variety of exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly compatible with most other mods. No major conflicts reported.
