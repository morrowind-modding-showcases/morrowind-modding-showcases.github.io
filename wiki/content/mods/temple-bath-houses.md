> Bathing comes to Vvardenfell! This mod adds 2 public bath houses to the world, one in Balmora and the other in Vivec. Each bath house has a merchant that will sell you towels, washclothes, brushes and soap or you can bring your own, especially if you use Provincial Bath Shoppe mod by Korana.

## World Edits

Adds a new interior cell to the Balmora Temple and Vivec Temple cantons. Makes very minor exterior edits to the Temple canton, namely a new entrance and nothing else. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly compatible with other mods and confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and Redesigned Vivec. Will require the Balmora Temple Redone compatibility version to work with that mod.
