> Living Balmora is a small vanilla-friendly mod that enhances the city of Balmora by adding subtle clutter, decorations, and environmental details.
>
> The goal of this mod is not to redesign Balmora or fill every empty corner, but to make the city feel a little more lived-in while preserving the original atmosphere and gameplay.
>
> Every addition uses vanilla assets and is intended to blend naturally with the base game.

## Features:

- Adds subtle environmental clutter throughout Balmora
- Uses vanilla assets only
- Preserves the original city layout
- No gameplay changes
- No quests
- No new NPCs
- Lore-friendly additions
- Compatible with OpenMW and the original Morrowind

## World Edits

Exterior-only clutter overhaul of the cells around Balmora. Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with other Balmora city overhaul mods. Compatibility will be variable depending on whether or not other mods change the layout of Balmora.
