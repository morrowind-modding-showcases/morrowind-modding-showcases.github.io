> Search for a scenic boat located between Wolverine Hall in Sadrith Mora and Tel Fyr.

## Features:

- A new house with a lot of storage space and a teleportation amulet (and some other nice things);
- New enemy to fight wearing a unique robe by Ruffin Vangarr;
- Added some flora to the island near the boat, no landscape edits.

## World Edits

Adds a new boat and location to the Zafirbel Bay with associated exterior edits. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods. No reported conflicts.

A version set on the Tamriel Rebuilt Mainland is [available here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/60034)
