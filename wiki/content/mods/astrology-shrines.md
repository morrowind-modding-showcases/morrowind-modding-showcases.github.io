> Adds three animated floating Astrology shrines across Vvardenfell which give temporary boosts to the player. Speed, attack and magicka.

## World Edits

Adds new exterior-only locations to three exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with some large terrain editing mods, but otherwise is unlikely to have any major conflicts.
