> Adds several new quests to the cultists of Mehrunes Dagon and makes them friendly as soon as you start the main quest of the mod.

## World Edits

Minor quest edits to a handful of interior and exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most mods that don't modify the layouts of various Daedric shrines. Reportedly has a minor conflict with Daedric Shrine Overhaul.
