> Travel to Khuul and investigate the rumors about a smuggling operation nearby. Or perhaps it's more than that?

## Features:

- a chance to discover the secrets of an underwater sanctuary
- a relatively short quest for mid-level characters
- a unique item to make your travels more pleasant
- a custom icon for Skyrim Style Quest Notifications

## World Edits

Adds two new dungeon locations in the West Gash with associated exterior clutter and new interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods.
