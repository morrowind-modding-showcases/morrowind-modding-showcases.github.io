Overhaul of the Ashlander Camps

## World Edits:

Overhauls and expands the four big Ashlander camps; Urshilaku, Zainab, Erabenimsun, and Ahemussa. Includes new interiors, exterior detailing and clutter, and landscape edits. As well as interior overhauls.

## Other Notes:

This mod has been merged into Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Otherwise, it's compatible with most other mods, including Finding the Erabenimsun Camp, The Song of the Grazelands, and OAAB Grazelands.

It also includes a merged version of Urshilaku Spiritual and will conflict with that mod as a result.

Conflicts with Native Lands.
