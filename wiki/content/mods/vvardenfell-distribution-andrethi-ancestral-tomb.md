> Makes modest, vanilla-friendly adjustments to the Andrethi Ancestral Tomb to add variety to both the funeral goods as well as enemies, namely turning the generic Berne Clan Vampire NPCs into named, unique NPCs with TD and OAAB items.

## World Edits

Lightly overhauls the interior of the Andrethi Ancestral Tomb. Makes NO exterior cell edits and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Comes with a compatibility patch for Andrethi  Tomb Overhaul by Jsp25. No other major conflicts reported, but will presumably conflict with mods altering the layout of the Andrethi Ancestral Tomb.
