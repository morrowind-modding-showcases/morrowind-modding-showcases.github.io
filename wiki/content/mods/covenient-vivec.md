> This is a very simple mod that reduces the rage and frustration you feel navigating through Vivec.
> It adds a lot of new bridges, so there are a lot more pathways to go from A to B, also on the second level.

## World Edits

Adds and moves a variety of objects in the exterior cells of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with most Vivec city overhauls.
