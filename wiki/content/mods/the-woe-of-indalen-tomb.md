Elmussa Damori has been suffering nightmares, perhaps you might offer her assistance?

## Summary

Overhauls Indalen ancestral tomb near Caldera and adds a quest to clear it out. Ask for rumours in Caldera and follow them to Elmussa who will explain the situation. Depending on how well you deal with the quest the reward may be access to a small player home.

## World Edits:

Adds a new, small, player home to Caldera (using an existing building) with minimal exterior edits. Also overhauls the Indalen Ancestral Tomb.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Includes a compatibility patch for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods, except potentially other Indalen Ancestral Tomb overhauls.
