Some minor cluttering and decorations atop the Grand Pharos of Seyda Neen, including a magical "Pharos Stone" that sends forth a beam of light day or night.

## World Edits:

Exterior clutter only overhaul of the Seyda Neen lighthouse.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Includes compatibility support for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Seyda Neen by the Nwahs and Mushrooms Team, and OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat.

Confirmed compatible with Shroomda Neen and Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees.

Will conflict with other lighthouse overhauls like Hlaalu Seyda Neen.
