- 14, -4

## Other Notes:

Mod has been merged into Beautiful Cities of Morrowind (in a lesser form) and consequently will conflict with BCOM unless using [Katya's Patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/56060).

Otherwise, compatible with The Mountain of Fear and Molag Amur Mountains.
