> Simple vanilla friendly changes to make the Thieves Guild quests better structured and more enjoyable.

## Features:

- The Redoran Manor jobs are merged into two quests, and two new quests have been added to Aengoth to make the story more interesting.
- There's a simple change to Sugar-Lips Habasi’s third quest, so that it's a bit more feasible for an early-game character to retrieve the Dwemer Artifacts. You should find the quest slightly more logical.
- One of the Redoran Manor quests was given by Big Helende. The original quest was also kind of silly in tone. I’ve merged that quest into Aengoth's, and a new quest more relevant to Sadrith Mora’s operation has replaced it.

## World Edits

Minor edits to interior and exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed compatible with BCOM, OAAB - Tombs and Towers, Velothi Towers Refit, and a patch is available for City of Ash. No known conflicts reported.
