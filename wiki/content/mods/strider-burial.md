Strider Burial is an overhaul of the area containing a few Strider husks East of Urshilaku Camp, turning the zone in a small Silt Strider graveyard complete with an optional high-level unmarked quest.

## World Edits:

Primarily a small landscape overhaul of the central northern Ashlands, adding new details, statics, and clutter to a handful of exterior cells. Includes landscape edits.

Makes NO edits to interior cells.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with most other mods in the northern Ashlands. No known conflicts reported.
