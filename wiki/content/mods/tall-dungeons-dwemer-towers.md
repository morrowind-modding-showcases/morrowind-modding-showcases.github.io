Includes two modules for both Dwemer ruins and Daedric shrines.

## Tall Dwemer Towers:

Adds taller Dwemer towers to the exteriors of nine Dwemer ruins on Vvardenfell.

## World Edits:
Edits the exteriors of the following Dwemer ruins:
- Nchurdamz
- Nchardumz
- Nchuleftingth
- Mzanchend
- Galom Daeus
- Mzahnch
- Nchuleft
- Bthuand
- Arkngthunch-Sturdumz

Note: Makes NO Landscape Edits!

## Other Notes:
Compatible with virtually everything, there have been no known conflicts documented.

Confirmed compatible with Dwemer Lightning Rods and OAAB Dwemer Pavements.

## Daedric Beacons:

Adds new beacons with associated architecture and light sources to the exteriors of Daedric shrines.

## World Edits:

Edits the exteriors of the following Daedric shrines:
- Kaushtarari
- Ainat
- Shashpilamat
- Esutanamus
- Kushtashpi
- Bal-ur
- Zergonipal
- Ashunartes
- Ebernanit
- Assarnatamat
- Onnissaralis
- Ramimilk
- Ashurnabitashpi
- Dushariran

Note: Makes NO Landscape Edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, no known conflicts have been reported.
