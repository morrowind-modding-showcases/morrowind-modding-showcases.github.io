Rebuilds the interior and exterior of the Caldera governors manor to be more imposing.

## World Edits:

Completely replaces the interior and exterior of the Caldera Governors Manor with new assets.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatibility patches are available for Calder by the Nwahs and Mushrooms Team, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Animated Morrowind, and Correspondences of Morrowind.

Confirmed compatible with Beyond the Firmament (with the latest version), The Mysterious Affair of Sara Shenk, Caldera Fighters Guild, Tamrielic Treasures, Caldera Priory, The Woe of Indalen Tomb, and most other minor Caldera mods.

Will conflict with Cozy Caldera (minor floating objects) and The Black Mill (scripted events are partially blocked by room dividers).
