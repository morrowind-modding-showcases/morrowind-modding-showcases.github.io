Visit the Land's End Tavern, a new location in the Bitter Coast Region south of Hla Oad. Listen to the bard's tale at night to discover a new quest about a cursed ship, The Silent Siren. This mod adds two new locations to the Bitter Coast and some new loot to find while seeking out the source of the cursed ship.

## World Edits:

Adds a couple of new locations to the Bitter Coast, includinga tavern south of Hla Oad, and a new island dungeon off the coastline.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, Hla Odai, and Hla Oad by the Nwahs and Mushrooms team. Presumably compatible with most other Bitter Coast mods. No known conflicts reported.
