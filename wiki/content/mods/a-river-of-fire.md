Floods Foyada Ashur-Dan with lava, with an optional addition of adding burning ash motes to all lava pools in Molag Amur.

## World Edits:

Adds terrain assets to the exterior cells of the Foyada Ashur-Dan, spanning the border of the Southern Ashlands and the Molag Amur Region. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, and confirmed compatible with From Wind Into Stone. Has a compatibility patch for Uvirith's Legacy.
