Join the Imperial Dwemer Society and help research the Dwemer. Use Dwemer machinery, create unique items, restore an old teleportation system, add new exhibits to the guild's museum...

## World Edits:

Quest mod that adds new quest objects, clutter, and NPCs to a variety of interior and exterior locations across Vvardenfell, including some minor expansions to existing Dwemer ruins.

Also adds a new exterior structure along the southern Odai River, along with landscape edits to that cell.

## Other Notes:

An improved compatibility version, without landscape edits, [is available here](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/57848). The original mod download also has a compatibility patch for the Dwemer Rebirth Project.

Otherwise, should be compatible with most mods, minus those that make landscape edits along the southern Odai River.
