A Hlaalu-run newspaper exclusively for Vvardenfell based on Tamriel
Rebuilt's Ebonheart Bellman and Canyon Echo. Adds the Vvardenfell on
Vellum printing office to **Vivec, Hlaalu Canalworks** and articles to inns throughout Vvardenfell.

"Spawners"
 (containers) for the newspaper can be found in or outside of just about
 any tradehouse/cornerclub in Vvardenfell. They replenish on cell
change, but I recommend not spamming them or you'll run through 'em
quickly.

A **new quest** can also be received that
regards the newspaper. You must be a Hlaalu member of adequate rank, and
 then speak to Velanda Omani about the "business" topic.

Creating
 an add-on/adding new issues that cover modded events is simple, as the
mod is an ESM and the newsletters are a leveled list.
