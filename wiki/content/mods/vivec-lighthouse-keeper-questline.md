> A 4-part questline that lets you assist the lighthouse keeper at the Vivec Lighthouse south of Ebonheart (requires mod by MwGek). Perform hard work for the keeper, weather a thunderstorm, and uncover a dark secret.

## World Edits

Adds quest-related items and objects to a variety of exterior and interior cells near Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. A patch is available for Vivec City by the Nwahs and Mushrooms team. Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods.
