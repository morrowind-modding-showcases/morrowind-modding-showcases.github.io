This mod populates the Shrine of Azura on the Azura's Coast with some pilgrims and a priestess along with some other edits.

## World Edits:

Edits the interior and exterior of the Shrine of Azura, adding lightweight new clutter and details.

## Other Notes:

Will likely conflict with other Shrine of Azura overhauls, but compatible with Azura's Coast Redone and most other Azura's Coast mods.
