> This expansion adds over 110 quests to all vampire clans and 7 new factions (5 subfactions of Aundae, 1 splinter faction of Quarra, 1 new clan - Ferver).

## Features:

- 110+ new quests: for the rogue/splinter sub-factions of the main Quarra and Aundae Clans, Berne Clan (v1.1.8+), and the new Ferver Clan; should be compatible with the old Quarra Clan mod,
- 1 dungeon (also lair of the new clan) with tough bosses and several levels (current count: 2), desired player level 29+, Daggerfall-style structure,
- New NPCs with some *fanfiction* lore, etc. I've tried to integrate them seamlessly into the existing geopolitical structure of Morrowind,
- Dynamic interior updates/changes (more functionality in the OpenMW version), some NPCs arrive as the story progresses,
- A new joinable vampire clan called "Ferver" (yes, almost like "fervor"),  new scripts, no edits to vanilla game ( quests like cure vampirism are detected, etc.),
- Powerful items (some have low drop chance, akin to the MMO loot systems), that are dropped by creatures/ can be found only inside the new dungeon,
- Some basic non-linear quest branching, although rather primitive, I'm often enhancing some of my previous quests in each new version,
- Wereboar NPC and disease (thx to Tamriel Data Masterfile). Why a wereboar? Because I love them, that's why; also makes a bit of sense story-wise,
- V1.1.7(+) integrated TelShadow's Aundae Dungeons, with new quests for that faction, added Kieve's Aundae Sentinel Armor,
- V1.1.7(+) integrated OperatorJack's The Weary Vampire mod

## World Edits

Large-scale quest mod adding new locations to a variety of exterior cells with associated landscape edits. Also makes edits to a number of vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Merges in [The Weary Vampire](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49774), [Excise Office Basement](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49442)﻿ and [Clan Aundae Vampire Dungeons](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/47292). May have conflicts with other mods making landscape edits to the same exterior cells.
