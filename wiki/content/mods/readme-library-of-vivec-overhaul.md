> This mod redesigns the Library of Vivec from scratch to make it look and feel like a true library, with many books added from Tamriel_Data and OAAB_Data for you to read. There are also new NPCs for you to interact with, and two new quests that set you on the hunt for a unique book the resource for which was provided by RubberMan.

## World Edits

Overhauls and expands the interiors of the Library of Vivec. Also adds three new locations, with a new building outside Maar Gan, and two new dungeon entrances with associated interiors in the Grazelands and Azura's Coast. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and should be compatible with most other mods that don't overhaul the Library of Vivec. Alternative Quest Only and Library Overhaul versions exist on the mod download page.
