> It enhances the area around Molag Mar by adding a new temple and a lighthouse with docks. Compatible with all mods that touch Molag Mar.

## Features:
- Scenic locations that will make your travels to Molag Mar more interesting. Welcome to Al'Mar, a temple that serves as a base for a special group of Bonesaint Ordinators who guard treacherous Molag Amur wastelands, and assist travelers in reaching sacred pilgrimage sites. 
- Molag Mar now has an additional dock with a lighthouse.
- New Bonesaint armors and weapons by Ruffin Vanagrr and Pulcharmsolis (plus a unique variant of the chest, helmet and a mace were added, find them inside the temple).
- Visit a new inn at the lower level of the temple. You can rent a room or purchase fancy quarters. You also can relax at the Sacred Spring and eat some pomegranates! 

## World Edits

Adds new locations with associated exterior detailing on the outskirts of Molag Mar, including new interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods overhauling or expanding Molag Mar.
