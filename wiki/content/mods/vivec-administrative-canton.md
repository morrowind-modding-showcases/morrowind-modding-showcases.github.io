> A mod created to touch up the High Fane, Justice Offices, Office of the Watch, Hall of Justice and Hall of Wisdom interiors with assets from OAAB and Tamriel Data.

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of the interiors of the Vivec Temple canton. Makes NO edits to exterior cells and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other Vivec city mods, including most other mods that overhaul the Vivec temple canton. Only exception is mods like Vivec - City of Swords and Vivec - God the City, which change the layout for the Temple canton and are thus likely to conflict.
