A well appointed player's home for sale.

## World Edits:

Depending on the version used, edits cells off the coast of either Seyda Neen, Ebonheart or Vivec with a new player home and associated interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits!

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, even with a theoretical conflict in one location, will be virtually guaranteed to be compatible in another location.

No known conflicts reported.

A [newer version of this player home](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50422) was released in 2021 with Cloud Storage support.
