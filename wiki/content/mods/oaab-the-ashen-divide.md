OAAB - The Ashen Divide is a mini OAAB release and a remake/adaptation of the original The Ashen Divide mod. It transforms the Ashlands region between Seyda Neen and Balmora into a unique region that merges the biomes of the Bitter Coast and the Ashlands utilizing unique assets. It also overhauls the bandit lair of Zainsipilu.

## World Edits:

Overhauls and edits the terrain between Seyda Neen and Hla Oad, adding new clutter and detailing, as well as landscape edits.

Also overhauls and expands the dungeon of Zainsipilu.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with some other Bitter Coast landscape overhaul mods, and possibly conflict with other overhauls of Zainsipilu, but otherwise is compatible with most mods in the Bitter Coast region.
