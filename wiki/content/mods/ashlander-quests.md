> This mod lets you do more quests for the Ashlander tribes. Ideal for roleplaying.

## Features:

- 11 new Ashlander quests (which is more than double of the amount that vanilla offers)
- They are little, roleplaying quests which help you feel more immersed with the Ashlander way of life: Help set up the ebony trade, hunt down dangerous beasts, help an Ashlander learn more about the outside world etc.
- An opportunity to advance in the "Ashlanders" faction
- 3 Urshilaku, 3 Zainab, 3 Erabenimsun quests + two quests for Ashlanders outside these camps (The Ahemmusa camp is left untouched so that the mod would be compatible with Julan, Ashlander Companion)
- Small rewards, and the feeling that the player's actions have an effect on the world of the game
- Clean, bug-free, lore friendly mod (Version 1.1. fixed that one bug it had, and cleaned that one vanilla dialogue entry which was accidentally dirtied.)

## World Edits

Very minor exterior cell edit, adding new quest-related object/NPC. Makes NO landscape edits. Primarily a dialogue mod.

## Other Notes

Likely compatible with almost everything. No known conflicts have been reported.
