## World Edits

Adds a new location with a new building, exterior clutter, and landscape edits just north of Ebonheart. Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Due to landscape edits, will likely conflict with a number of Ebonheart overhauls. Building location is also fairly popular and may have other conflicts.
