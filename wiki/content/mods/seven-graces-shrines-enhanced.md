Each shrine has been redesigned and enhanced to make them look like important Temple pilgrimage sites.

Each shrine has been decorated with items which reflect their purpose and offerings - such as soul gem piles around the Shrine of Pride.

The Puzzle Canal Center has been cluttered extensively, making the place look more visually interesting and fun to explore.

The area outside Koal Cave now has some docks and decorations, to indicate the presence of a pilgrimage site.

Designed with compatibility in mind - edits have been minimal with no landscape edits being made.

## World Edits:

Primarily a clutter and detail overhaul focusing on the interiors and exterior locations of various Tribunal shrines associated with the Seven Graces.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Modular options are available for players to pick and choose which shrine overhauls they want. Some shrine locations will have conflicts with other overhauls, but due to the modular nature, Seven Graces is highly compatible.
