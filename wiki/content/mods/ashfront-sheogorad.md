Ashfront - Sheogorad is an overhaul of the entirety of the Sheogorad region, transforming this afterthought of development into a unique environment of black sand beaches, geothermal activity, and Nordic settlement. As opposed to more arctic-themed overhauls, I've considered instead proximity to the Ashlands and implied geothermal activity in creating a verdant, yet rugged region where warmth and ash from Red Mountain collide with the cool waters and winds of the Sea of Ghosts. While this environment allows for familiar mycology to bloom, its unique climate has forced some degree of adaptation, developing a unique breed of Emperor Parasol reminiscent of those from Skyrim's Dragonborn DLC, along with the native Sootspire mushroom.

Courtesy of RandomPal and mwgek, Nordic Dagon Fel and Dagon Fel Lighthouse serve as the basis in better reflecting the culture of the Nordic inhabitants of the region's only town, and the settlement of their ancient ancestors is reflected in haunting ruins scattered about the islands. Calderas on the main island have had their heights raised, greater detail has been given to many minor locations, and new points of interest added both above ground and underwater, where hydrothermal vents allow for pockets of life to thrive in otherwise frigid conditions.

Released as part of the Ashfront project, an effort to diversify the environs of Vvardenfell in a vanilla-friendly fashion through expansion of existing themes, implementation of prerelease concepts, improved detailing, and addition of points of interest.

## World Edits:

Makes extensive edits to exterior cells, including the town of Dagon Fel, and wide-ranging landscape edits that cover the entirety of the Sheogorad region and the cells for some bordering regions of the Ashlands, Azura's Coast and Grazelands.

Also includes interior overhauls for the town of Dagon Fel.

## Other Notes:

Includes compatibility patches for OAAB Shipwrecks and Immersive Grotto Entrances.

See [Ashfront Patch Collection](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59601) for additional compatibility patches.

Merges Nordic Dagon Fel, Dagon Fel Lighthouse, and Sanctus Shrine.

Will likely conflict with most mods set in the Sheogorad.
