This mod makes West Gash more rocky.

## World Edits:

Major landscape overhaul, covering virtually the entire West Gash region with new terrain assets and landscape edits.

Number of cells covered varies by version, see alternates.

## Alternative Versions:

A number of alternative versions exist for Rocky West Gash that make it more compatible with other mods, including:
- [Devilish Rocky West Gash](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/53420)
- [BCOM Rocky West Gash](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50565)
- [Rocky West Gash Aggressively Compatible Version](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50161)

Using one of these versions will ensure compatibility with nearly all mods impacting the West Gash. Any conflicts in any other versions are usually covered by one of the alternates. See coverage map of variants to see the differences on the TES 3 Mod Map.
