Adds a travellers rest stop to the West Gash, between Gnisis and Caldera.

## World Edits:

Adds a new tavern/waypoint just north of the Salothran Ancestral Tomb, with associated exterior clutter and new interior.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Requires Rocky West Gash or one of its variants. Otherwise, has no known conflicts.
