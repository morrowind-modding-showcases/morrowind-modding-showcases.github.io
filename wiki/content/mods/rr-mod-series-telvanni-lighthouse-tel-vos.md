This mod adds a Telvanni style Lighthouse not far from Tel Vos and Tel Mora.

## World Edits:

Adds a new location off the Azura's Coast with new terrain assets, cluttering, and landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with OAAB Tel Mora, OAAB Grazelands, OAAB Shipwrecks, and most other mods. Note, a version of this mod was merged into Morrowind Rebirth.
