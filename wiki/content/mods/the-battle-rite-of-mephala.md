> On a strange island known to few as Crescent Moon Isle, a bloodbath awaits the player in a struggle for survival against a band of crazed assassins, all for the amusement of a few cultists hiding away in the shadows. Partake in the slaughter with joy, or perhaps you believe in following the deity's actual wishes, hidden among the bodies...

## Features:

- Intense "Battle Royale" gameplay on an unforgiving island
- Randomly placed npcs and loot - ensuring no experience is ever the same
- A hidden solution to fulfill Mephala's wishes...
- Heavy lua scripting to allow NPC AI and gameplay like you've never experienced in Morrowind before
- You're in it to win it, so fight dirty! Instead of swords and maces you must kill with sticks, shovels, frying pans, shards of glass, and so forth!
- The Ebony Blade, a powerful weapon obtained should you prove useful to Mephala. Equipped with a powerful enchantment that powers the blade as you murder!

## World Edits

Adds a new landmass to the far northwest of Vvardenfell, including landscape edits, and new buildings and locations with associated interiors,. Makes a minor edit to the Black Shalk Cornerclub in Vivec.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, minus those adding a landmass to the same location.
