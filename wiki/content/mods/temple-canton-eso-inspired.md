> This mod overhauls the Vivec, Temple Canton exterior with an inspiration of the ESO Morrowind version, adding a few planters of trees and mushrooms and added lights as well as filling up the once largely empty space that the Temple Canton is known for, also lowering the one Vivec statue so its better to look at from the ground.

## Features:

- Planters
- Displayed Trees And Mushrooms
- Added A few Bits Of Detailing
- More Lights
- Chairs And Benches To Make It Looked More Visited

## World Edits

Overhauls the Vivec Temple canton, including interior edits. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with other mods overhauling the Vivec Temple canton.
