> A lore-friendly quest mod based on an in-game book brought to life, with dungeons, mind-bending puzzles, quests, Daedra, and the power to summon items from oblivion.

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Sheogorad region, along with associated exterior clutter and detailing. Also adds a new quest-related object to Jobasha's Rare Books in Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly compatible with most other mods, including Spines of Madness.
