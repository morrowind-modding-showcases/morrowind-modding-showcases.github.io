> A cluttered overhaul of Balmora's Temple, designed with compatibility in mind, using assets from OAAB and TR_Data.

## Features:

- Clutter at the top walkway of the Velothi dome (compatible with Throat-Singing Temple Singers)
- Narrative clutter in the main upper temple - an area where Paupers are educated by the Temple as mentioned in their My Trade topic, for instance
- More offerings at shrines and ash pits
- More personal clutter in temple attendant's rooms
- A lot of funerary urns in the lower floor crypt, as well as other ceremonial items and displays
- A reading corner, with several scrolls of temple scripture to study. These have been written by me, some are brand new for this mod, some are from my Skyrim mod Apocryphal Library

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of the Balmora temple, adding new clutter, detailing and assets.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, including likely the compatibility version of Balmora Temple Redone and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
