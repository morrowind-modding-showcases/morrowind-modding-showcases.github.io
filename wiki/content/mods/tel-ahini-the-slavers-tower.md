## Description
The slavers spire is an interesting little mod from the 2022 Winter Modjam. However upon playing it, I was rather underwhelmed with how little there was there. Only serving as a landmark.

Fortunately, the perms were open, and I took it upon myself to flesh it out and make the areas surrounding it far more interesting.

## Main features
Rather than just being a backdrop, the spire now contains some loot, enemies, and a new house pod. Giving you a compelling reason to explore the tower. In addition, this mod gives a facelift to the nearby Bensiberib Camp, and cave of Ahinipalit.

There is more detail here than in the original mod, and much more visual story telling.

## World Edits:

Adds a new dungeon location with exterior clutter and detailing to the edge of the Molag Amur Region, along the coast of the Zafirbel Bay. Includes a new associated dungeon interior. Also overhauls the dungeon of Ahinipalit.

## Other Notes:

Should be compatible with most mods, but will presumably conflict with The Slavers Spire, which has been merged into this mod.
