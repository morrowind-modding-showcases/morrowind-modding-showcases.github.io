A new Bonemold smith has opened shop in Aldrhun.

## World Edits:

Adds a new building with associated interior in Ald'ruhn with some exterior clutter. Also overhauls the dungeon of Mallapi.

Includes a new quest and associated dialogue.

## Other Notes:

Compatibility patches are available for Ald'ruhn - City of Ash, Ald'ruhn - Seat of Power, and Ald'ruhn by the Nwahs and Mushrooms Team.

Will possibly conflict with other Ald'ruhn city overhauls.
