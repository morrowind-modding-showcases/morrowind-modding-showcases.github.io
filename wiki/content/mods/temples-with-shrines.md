> So in Morrowind there are numerous temples that don't have any shrines inside. This is both weird and frustrating, as when you are trying to get that disease cured, you enter the temple and bam. No shrine. This mod fixes that and adds shrines to the temples in Maar Gan, Molag Mar, Suran and Vos.

## World Edits

Interior-only mod adding new shrines to various temple interiors. Makes NO landscape or exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Will have variable compatibility with other mods overhauling the temples of Maar Gan, Molag Mar, Suran and Vos.
