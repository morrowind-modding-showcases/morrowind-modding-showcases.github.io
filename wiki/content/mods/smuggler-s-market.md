Pirates upon the shore of Azura's Coast, along with a market, tavern, and small cavern. Includes one short quest.

## World Edits:

Adds a small settlement to the exterior cells of the Azura's Coast bordering the Molag Amur region, with exterior buildings, clutter, and associated new interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits and no edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods. No known conflicts reported.
