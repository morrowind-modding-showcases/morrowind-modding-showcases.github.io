Adds a gondola network between Sadrith Mora, Tel Aruhn, and Tel Fyr, as well as a small fishing boat north of Tel Aruhn. Makes use of some excellent Telvanni boat models by DimNussens.

## World Edits:

Adds new exterior additions to the docks of the towns of the Zafirbel Bay region, including a new ship off the coast of Tel Aruhn, and other minor exterior edits.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, and has no known mod conflicts.
