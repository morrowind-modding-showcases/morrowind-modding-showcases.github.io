In vanilla Morrowind, a lonely dock and a bridge connecting two small islands, stood without rhyme or reason, until now; speak with Raflod the Braggart for more information. Smuggler's Run does not only add a two story shack for player's to use, but also improves upon Bethesda's bland exterior landscaping as well. 

The shack is not a luxury suite, nor a cheat house; expect to spent sometime cleaning the mess and rearranging items. Although it's not a hard requirement, it's highly suggested that Perfect Placement be used in conjunction with this mod.

## Features:
- A two story shack 
- plenty of storage space and new items
- improved landscape (two small islands east of Seyda Neen)

## World Edits:

Adds a new building to the island south of Seyda Neen, complete with landscape edits to cells (-1, -10) and (-1, -11).

## Other Notes:

Compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, and OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat IF loaded AFTER those mods. Otherwise, will conflict.

May have additional conflicts due to landscape edits.
