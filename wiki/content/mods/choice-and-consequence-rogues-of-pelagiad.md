> This mod adds several new options and tweaks for dealing with the two rogues at Pelagiad's Halfway Tavern.

## World Edits

Minor edits to two interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, minus potentially those that change the layout of the Fort Pelagiad South Wall.
