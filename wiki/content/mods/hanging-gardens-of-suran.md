Revolutionize your gaming experience with "Hanging Gardens of Suran" - the mesmerizing mod that infuses the heart of Suran with awe-inspiring vertical gardens!

## World Edits:

Primarily a clutter overhaul, adding new objects to the exterior cells of Suran, mostly in the form of new hanging planters.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Contains compatibility patches for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Suran - Pearl of the Ascadian Isles, and Dallara Suran.

Highly compatible with most other mods, including most other Suran overhauls and expansions.

No known conflicts.
