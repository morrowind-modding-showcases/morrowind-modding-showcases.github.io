Help an eccentric Telvanni smith with his experiment and uncover the secrets of the Daedric Realm of Flame Atronachs. Two intertwined quests with scripted events, an overhaul of an otherwise generic vanilla location, new creatures and new equipment as a reward. Compatible with OpenMW. Part of the May Modathon Month 2024.
