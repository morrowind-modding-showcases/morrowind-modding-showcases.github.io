This mod adds a house to the southwest of Tel Branora.

## World Edits:

Adds a new Dwemer ruin off the coast of Tel Branora, along with detailing in the surrounding cells.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with Tethered Tel Branora, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Tel Branora Expansion, Tel Branora - Domain of the Mad Mistress, RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse, and Azura's Coast Redone.

No Known Conflicts have been Reported.
