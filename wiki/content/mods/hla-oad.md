Detailing Hla Oad to make this village more habitable. More defined boundaries. Fences! At the same time, we wanted to show Hla Oad to the poor village, as Camonna Tong intended.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of Hla Oad, including new clutter and detailing, as well as landscape edits and replacing some buildings with new assets.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees and Hla Odai. Will conflict with other Hla Oad overhauls.
