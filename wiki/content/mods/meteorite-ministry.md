## Description

The Ministry of Truth is supposed to be a meteorite that was bound to destroy the southern part of Vvardenfell. It is only by the power of Vivec himself that this meteorite is now suspended in mid-air, postponing the iminent destruction of this region.

In vanilla Morrowind however, the Ministry doesn't show much of its dramatic nature.
What this mod does, is adding a tail of smaller rocks to the Ministry, giving it a more dramatic and moving-object-suspended-in-mid-flight look. 

2 options for direction: towards the palace or towards the temple. Additionally, you get the option to elevate the Ministry higher in the air. B

ethesda originally kept it really low, because of the limited drawing distance back in 2002. With distand land, there's no reason to keep it this low anymore.

4 versions of the mod are available:
- Meteorite Ministry Palace: Baar Dau aimed at the palace, vanilla height
- Meteorite Ministry Palace - Higher: Baar Dau aimed at the palace, elevated higher in the sky
- Meteorite Ministry Temple: Baar Dau aimed at the temple, vanilla height
- Meteorite Ministry Palace - Temple: Baar Dau aimed at the temple, elevated higher in the sky

## World Edits:

Replaces the Ministry of Truth and moves it, depending on which version is used. Makes interior adjustments for door markers.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with all other mods EXCEPT other Ministry of Truth/Baar Dau overhauls.
