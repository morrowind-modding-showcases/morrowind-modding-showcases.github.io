> This mod was inspired by the theme *Fables & Foibles* for the [Morrowind May Modathon](https://forums.nexusmods.com/topic/13513211-the-2025-morrowind-may-modathon-month-modding-competition-celebrating-23-years-of-morrowind/). It adds a short Temple questline to Balmora given by [Dralval Andrano](https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Morrowind:Dralval_Andrano), the apothecary. You must be a member of the Tribunal Temple faction to begin.
>
> During the questline, you will have the opportunity to test an unusual potion, the Potion of Animal Articulation, that enables communication with animals. Through conversing with the local friendly wildlife—guar, scrib, and netch—you will be able to lend a hand to several creatures in need.
>
> There are five major quests and a sixth minor quest that sends you to the Ald'ruhn Temple for another similar-level questline. Being based out of Balmora, expect to explore some familiar and not so familiar locales in the surrounding area. The questline is suitable for low-level characters, especially if completed in the order below:
>
> - Broken Apparatus
> - Animal Articulation
> - Guar Troubles
> - The Scrib and the Nix-hound
> - A Netch in Molag Amur
> - The Road to Ald'ruhn

## World Edits

Makes minor edits, namely placing quest-related NPCs, creatures and/or objects, to a variety of interior and exterior locations. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly compatible with most other mods and confirmed compatible with Balmora Temple Redone and Balmora Temple Questline. No major conflicts reported.
