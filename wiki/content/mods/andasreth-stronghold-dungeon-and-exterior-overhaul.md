Rebuilds Andasreth stronghold and its exterior area.

## World Edits:

Features expanded and rebuilt dungeon interiors for Andasreth, as well as an exterior overhaul with a number of landscape edits and new terrain assets.

## Other Notes:

Conflicts with Better Dunmer Strongholds and The Northern Strongholds, but otherwise is compatible with most other mods, including the various edited versions of Rocky West Gash and Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees.

May have a conflict with Justice for Khartag (J.F.K.).
