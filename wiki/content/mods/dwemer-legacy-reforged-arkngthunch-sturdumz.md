Arkngthunch-Sturdumz was once a beacon of Dwemer ingenuity, a place where brilliant minds crafted wonders of unparalleled sophistication. Yet, its halls now stand silent, abandoned after a cataclysmic event that erased its occupants from history. 

Whispers of its existence have resurfaced, drawing adventurers eager to unravel its mysteries and claim its treasures. As you delve into the forgotten depths, you will face unimaginable dangers and uncover secrets long buried. 

But beware— Arkngthunch-Sturdumz does not easily yield its treasures, and those who enter may never return. Prepare yourself for an unforgettable journey into the heart of Dwemer mystery and danger. 

Will you uncover the secrets of the forgotten depths, or will you become yet another lost soul in the annals of Arkngthunch-Sturdumz?

## Features:

- Atmospheric Enhancements: The dark and foreboding halls of Arkngthunch-Sturdumz are brought to life with dynamic lighting, haunting shadows, and ambient sounds that evoke a sense of dread and curiosity.
- Unique Dwemer Artifacts - Unearth powerful and mysterious artifacts hidden within the ruins. These relics, infused with ancient Dwemer magic, hold secrets waiting to be discovered.
- New Enemies: Face off against fearsome new foes, including enhanced Dwemer constructs, rogue automatons, and spectral guardians that protect long forgoten secrets.
- Epic Boss Encounters: Confront formidable bosses that guard the deepest recesses of Sturdumz. These battles will test your skills and resolve to the limit.
- New events, deadly traps and more! 
- Stunning Visuals: Experience the breathtaking beauty of Dwemer architecture, with detailed clutter, intricate designs, and awe-inspiring structures that capture the essence of Dwemer ingenuity.

## World Edits:

Complete overhaul and replacement of the dungeon interior of Arkngthunch-Sturdumz.

Also makes extensive exterior and landscape edits to the cells surrounding the Dwemer ruin in the West Gash.

## Other Notes:

Will likely indirectly conflict with any other mods overhauling Arkngthunch-Sturdumz.
