I love the Vivec Lighthouse and its quest mod, but the walk to/from the Lighthouse and Ebonheart was a little boring. So I decided to change that.

## World Edits:

Adds flora and clutter detailing to the exterior cells between Ebonheart and the southern end of the peninsula across from Vivec.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and confirmed conflict with Ascadia Manor. Otherwise, should be compatible with the Great Sea Wall of Vivec, Vivec Lighthouse, Ebonheart Lighthouse, Road to the Lighthouse, and most other mods in the area.
