Epic Sadrith Mora does something unconventional; it looks at how future Bethesda games have crafted cities and tries to bring those teachings back to Morrowind. How it's done is by creating the illusion of a bigger city through the establishment of architecture surrounding vanilla buildings you interact with.

What you'll experience is a much denser Sadrith Mora that really makes use of the land mass it was created on.

## World Edits:

Large city overhaul mod that edits the cells of Sadrith Mora and Wolverine Hall adding new buildings, clutter, and detailing. Also makes landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

This mod has since been merged into or used as a base for Sadrith Mora - Seat of Power, Sadrith Mora BCOM Plus Module, and Zafirbel Beacons.

It'll conflict with most large Sadrith Mora overhauls, but is surprisingly compatible with many smaller Sadrith Mora mods.
