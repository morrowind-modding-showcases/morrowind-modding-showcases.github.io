Adds more personality to the lair of everybody's favorite Telvanni sorcerer, Divayth Fyr, by spreading some Tamriel_Data assets and making better use of the space that was given without expanding the tower too much. Experiment rooms, a great library, magical oddities and more awaits in the Tower of Tel Fyr.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors of Tel Fyr, including the Corprusarium.

A version without any edits to the Corprusarium is available for greater compatibility with various Corprusarium overhauls.

## Other Notes:

Will presumably conflict with Sorrow Heart of Tel Fyr, but likely compatible with most other mods.
