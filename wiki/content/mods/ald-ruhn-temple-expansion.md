> This mods expands the Ald-Ruhn Temple, adding two new buildings into a walled complexed connected to the original temple building.
> There are no new quests or unique dialogue, nor are there any spectacular items for you to find. This mod is, for all intents and purposes, and atmospheric expansion mod.

## World Edits

Overhaul and expansion of the Ald'ruhn Temple, including both the interior and exterior. Includes landscape edits.

## Other Notes

This mod has been merged into various Ald'ruhn city overhaul mods, as well as inspired similar overhauls and expansions of the Ald'ruhn temple. As such, is likely to conflict with most other Ald'ruhn city overhauls. A compatibility patch for The Doors - Ald'ruhn is included in the mod download.
