> There must be something in the water... Everyone who drinks out of a certain well in Pelagiad ends up sick! Started as part of the 2023 Morrowind Summer Modjam, and then became so much more.

## Description:

Featuring two new dungeons (one small, one big) with over 2,000 objects hand-placed objects between them; two new quests, one of which was scripted and partially written by DetailDevil; a custom Peryite mesh and texture by DimNussens; a voiced Peryite courtesy of Hurdrax Custos﻿; a new merchant who buys/sells miscellaneous goods; and a place to call your own if you really wanted to!

To start the quest, ask about the latest rumors in Pelagiad. To end the quest, talk to Ygfa, the Imperial Cult lady in Fort Pelagiad.

## World Edits

Adds a single new location outside of Pelagiad, with a handful of exterior cell references. Otherwise, makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with just about everything, no major conflicts reported.
