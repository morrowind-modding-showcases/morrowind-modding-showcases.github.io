> This mod adds a new docking area in the western part of Ebonheart, including a new warehouse.
> No quests or new dialogue. Just a scenic expansion.

## World Edits

Adds new buildings, ships, and associated interiors to the western side of Ebonheart. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with most Ebonheart city overhauls, but theoretically compatible with those that don't change the shape or layout of the city much.
