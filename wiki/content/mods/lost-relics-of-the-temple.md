> Holy relics of the Temple were lost to the minions of Dagoth Ur, and have since been corrupted by his influence. Reclaim these artifacts, cleanse them of their curses, and restore them to their true power!

## Features:
- Moves the location for the quest Crosier of St. Llothis the Pious to Endusal instead of Dagoth Ur. Now the player is no longer expected to trek into the literal end-game dungeon to complete a faction quest.
- Grants the player the ability to break the "curses" that the 6th House have placed on these holy relics. (After all, what else but a curse explains how rubbish these item's enchantments are!?)
- Learn more of the lore behind these relics (Fables), and restore them to their previous glory -- eliminating their current negative effects (Foibles)!

## World Edits

Makes a single edit to Endusal and one other interior cell. Makes NO landscape edits and NO exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Primarily a quest mod, and consequently, should be compatible with most other mods. A Morrowind Quests Redux version is available from the mod downloads page.
