> A small visual improvement of Ebonheart turning it into a semblance of an old castle.

## Mod Additions:

- added elements of the destroyed wall at the entrances to the town;
- added moss, vine, and ivy to the fort walls;
- added a small fishing pier and several boats;
- railings added in some places;
- added imperial flags;
- added flower beds;
- added a small flower garden near the tavern;
- added warehouses for crates and barrels;
- small boat and ship decoration;
- only an exteriors;
- path grid fixed, plugin cleared.

## World Edits

Exterior detail overhaul of Ebonheart and surroundings. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with most other Ebonheart overhauls, but should be compatible with most minor Ebonheart expansions.
