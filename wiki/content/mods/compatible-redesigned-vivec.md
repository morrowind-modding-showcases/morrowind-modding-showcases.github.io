## World Edits

Extensive overhaul and expansion of the city of Vivec, including new structures, clutter, detailing, and extensive landscape edits.

## Other Notes

This is a merged version of Redesigned Vivec with Vivec Waistworks Expansion, designed for compatibility with most Vivec Palace and Temple mods. Will conflict with any other Vivec city overhauls.
