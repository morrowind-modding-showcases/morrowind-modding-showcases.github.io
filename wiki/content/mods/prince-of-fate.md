> Become the servant of Hermaeus Mora and claim the legendary Oghma Infinium for yourself. This mod adds a questline for the daedric prince Hermaeus Mora, as well as a new shrine dedicated to him in the Sea of Ghosts. To start this mod, ask for the latest rumors in Dagon Fel. The Oghma Infinium functionality of this mod requires MWSE.

## World Edits

Adds a new shack to the Sheogorad, as well as a new dungeon to the bottom of the Sea of Ghosts, with associated interiors and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have a few landscape conflicts with other mods, but should otherwise be compatible with most mods in the Sheogorad region.
