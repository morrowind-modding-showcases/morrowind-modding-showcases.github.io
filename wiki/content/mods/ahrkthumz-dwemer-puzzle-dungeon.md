Ahrkthumz, a Dwemer ruin north of Caldera, has remained untouched since the last days of the Dwemer. Now, an eccentric researcher is on the cusp of unlocking its secrets. Venture deep into the ruins to discover a new Dwemer artifact, using a unique color-based puzzle mechanic to open doors and unlock new areas of the dungeon.

## Features:
- A new Dwemer dungeon north of Caldera, featuring expanded Dwemer tileset pieces from Tamriel_Data and OAAB_Data.
- A custom color-based puzzle mechanic to open doors. While not particularly mind-bending, hopefully the puzzles are still fun!
- A unique Dwemer artifact with three possible enchantments that you can switch between, as well as an illustrated book to display.
- A short quest involving the eccentric Khajiit researcher Ma'Dar and his desire to learn the ruin's secrets.

## World Edits:

Adds a new dungeon north of Caldera with some exterior detailing.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Caldera Priory, Tamrielic Treasures, Caldera Fighters Guild, and Rocky West Gash. No known conflicts reported.
