> Many tombs in Vanilla MW already contain many of the 36 lessons of vivec books, this mod fills in the gaps allowing you to find them all inside tombs. (23 books added to 23 tombs.)

## World Edits

Adds books to various ancestral tombs. Makes NO landscape and NO exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most other mods, but may have minor conflicts with various tomb overhauls (this will be highly variable depending on the type of tomb overhaul used).
