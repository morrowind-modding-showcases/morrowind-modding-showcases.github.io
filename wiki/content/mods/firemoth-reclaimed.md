This mod overhauls the Firemoth Fort quest (one of the official Bethesda plugins), redesigning it from ground up. Battle hordes of the undead, the violent weather, and your own sanity to figure out a way into a lair of a powerful lich who holds a famed artifact.

Feel up to the challenge? Ask around Seyda Neen for rumors and let your journey into the realm of darkness begin!

Features

- Redesigned Firemoth Fort, both exterior and interior
- Custom shaders, animations, meshes, sounds, and music
- Undead diversity thanks to Tamriel Data and OAAB
- Highly atmospheric venture into a failed Legion incursions on the fort
- Features a cameo of one of the other MMM 2022 mods, can you find it?
- Meant for high-end characters, best tackled after the Main Quest and official expansions

Morrowind Modding Madness 2022 - 5th Place Winning Entry

Note: Not Compatible with Tamriel Rebuilt's Fort Firemoth, or any other Firemoth overhaul.
