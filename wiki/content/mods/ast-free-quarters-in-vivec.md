> A small free flat in the Vivec, Foreign Quarter Canton, opposite of the Black Shalk Cornerclub. Good as a starter home for the weary adventurer with a light pocket. Prime real estate for any Vivec lovers!

## World Edits

Adds a new interior cell connected to the Foreign Quarter Waistworks. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, minus those that change the layout of or replace the Foreign Quarter Waistworks.
