A hermit on an island east of Vivec.
Befriend him and help him uncover the truth.
Or not.

## Features:
- a questline about Galen, a former priest of the Temple, now hermit on an island.
- read Galen's journal.
- branching quests with choices and variations that intertwine with the main quest.
- an overhaul of a tiny island east of Vivec that you can call home if you'd like.﻿

## World Edits:

Edits two exterior cells in the Norvayn Bay, including cluttering, detailing, flora and terrain asset additions, as well as landscape edits.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Extremely compatible with other mods, including virtually all Vivec city overhauls, the Great Sea Wall of Vivec, Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage, and OAAB Shipwrecks.
