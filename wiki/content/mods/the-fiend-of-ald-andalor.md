Explore an overhauled and expanded Andalor Ancestral Tomb and conquer the evil that lies within.

﻿## Overview:

An old family prophecy, long thought to be nothing more than a myth, of the Andalor clan has begun to unfold as the very dead have begun to arise from their graves within the Andalor Ancestral tomb. Explore the tomb and hunt the horrors that lie within.

## Mod Features:

- An overhauled and expanded Andalor Ancestral Tomb
- A landscape overhaul for the tomb's exterior
- The ruins of the Ald Andalor Inn, the long abandoned business of the Andalor family
- A brief quest with multi-branching dialogue
- A multi-stage boss battle with the tomb's chief inhabitant
- Role-playing options for dialogue, like being a necromancer, a punk-rocker (yes I am), or a total nerd
- A renewable source of black soul gems, for the necromanticly inclined

## World Edits:

Overhauls the Andalor Ancestral Tomb, also adds landscape and terrain detailing to the associated exteriors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with nearly all other southern Grazelands mods, including The Song of the Grazelands and The Vanishing Ash-Chirps.
