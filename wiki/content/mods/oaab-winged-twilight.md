Meet Ari-Ari, a Winged Twilight who will gladly accompany you on your adventures. Ari-Ari can be found in her house somewhere north of Telasero, the Dunmer Stronghold located to the east of Suran. The mod also features a visual overhaul of Azura’s Shrine using OAAB assets and a new daedric ruin.

## World Edits:

Adds new locations to the Molag Amur region, with ruins, clutter, and detailing to the north of Mount Kand, as well as a new Daedric shrine in the same general area.

Also overhauls the interior of the Shrine of Azura, and makes edits to the exterior of the Shrine of Azura as well.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Has compatibility patches for Molag Amur Mountains and Azura's Shrine Overhaul.

Otherwise, mostly compatible with other mods.
