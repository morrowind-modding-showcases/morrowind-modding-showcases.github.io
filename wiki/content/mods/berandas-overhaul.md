This mod overhauls and slightly expands the old Dunmer fortress Berandas near Gnisis. Daedra have claimed Berandas, but other than the few roaming Daedra themselves there is not much evidence of the corruption. With this mod, old daedric structures have risen from the deep to transform the fortress into a proper daedric stronghold.

The mod adds daedric structures to the Berandas exterior cell and to the first two levels of the interior. It also makes some slight changes to their layouts. The last cell, the underground, has been redone from scratch. There are also two new, additional cells, although one of them is really tiny. The other one not so much. As of Version 2.1 there are also some clues as to what might be the cause of the daedric corruption as well as a tiny quest to go along with them.

Note that there are more daedric creatures walking the old corridors and caverns than in vanilla, so it has gotten a little harder to reach those boots. If your character can handle one or two golden saints, you should be fine, though.

## World Edits:

Complete overhaul and expansion of the interiors of Berandas. Also makes exterior static and clutter additions.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with Better Dunmer Strongholds.

Otherwise should be compatible with most other mods, including Rocky West Gash and Heart of the Velothi - Gnisis.
