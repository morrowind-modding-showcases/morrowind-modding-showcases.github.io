Lightweight overhaul of Seyda Neen that adds planters with glowing mushrooms and some clutter. Very compatible and performance friendly. Also adds a trader who sells stylish mushroom hats made by Ruffin Vangarr﻿.

## World Edits:

A clutter-only overhaul of central Seyda Neen. Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits.

A lightweight option for even better compatibility is available on the mod page.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with the following mods:
- Beautiful Cities of Morrowind by RandomPal
- Shabby Seyda Neen﻿ by Dimnussens and Lucevar
- Seyda Neen﻿ by Nwahs and Mushrooms Team (with lightweight option)
- OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat﻿ by Lucevar (with lightweight option)
- Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees by Melchior Dahrk
- Pharos Burns to Light Their Way by Kalinter

Merges Arrille's Garden and will conflict with the stand-alone version of that mod.

Will conflict with Hlaalu Seyda Neen.
