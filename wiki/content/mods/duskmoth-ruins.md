An imperial style set of ruins south and east of Suran. There's a minor quest involved starting with a knight who's further southeast down the coast from the ruins. No landscape changes where made.

## World Edits:

New exterior location in the Molag Amur region with a set of buildings and associated new dungeon interiors. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, no known conflicts reported.
