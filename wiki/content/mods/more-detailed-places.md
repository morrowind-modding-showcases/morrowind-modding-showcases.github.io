## World Edits

Extensive city overhaul mod, covering most settlements and locations in the game, including exterior and interior detailing and clutter, new buildings and associated interiors, and widespread landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly incompatible with most other city overhaul and landscape overhaul mods for Morrowind. Elements of this mod have also been incorporated into newer city overhaul mods. More modular versions of these city overhauls exist for download on the Nexus.
