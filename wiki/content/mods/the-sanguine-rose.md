> The Sanguine Rose adds a fully featured Daedric Quest for Sanguine, the prince of hedonistic revelry, debauchery, and passionate indulgences of darker natures. An Ordinator has been sniffing out Sanguine's shrine and he wants you to take care of the nosy do-gooder in an unconventional way.

## Features:

- New Daedric artifact: the Sanguine Rose
- Sanguine Rose summons a random Lesser Daedra including Herne, Xivilai, and Dark Seducers
- New statue for Sanguine in the style of the vanilla statues to fit seamlessly into the game
- Immersive voice acting for Sanguine courtesy of LondonRook
- New shrine to Sanguine includes revelers with unique dialogue and greetings
- New multi-path pacifist Daedric quest for Sanguine
- Includes option to betray Sanguine to the ordinators, witness and profit from the consequences

## World Edits

Expands the interior of the Balmora, Lucky Lockup, as well as adds new quest NPCs and objects to a variety of vanilla interiors and exteriors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods.
