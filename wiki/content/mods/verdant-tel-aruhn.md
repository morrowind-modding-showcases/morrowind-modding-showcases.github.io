A small and productive replacer of Tel Aruhn, inspired by thoughts of the ancient Merethic era.

## Features:
- marshmerrows and seashells on the coast;
- a place for recreation and fishing on the shore;
- green beautiful lanterns (I recommend a lovely replayer from Melchior Dahrk);
- flora, grass, giant mushrooms and moss all over the tower area;
- few containers;
- decorated pleasure boats;
- Archmagister Gothren's  private pier and boat;
- fixed the super-broken default architecture of the tavern root system. Now the root models don't stick out of the building. In this regard the position of the sign is slightly moved;
- some minor architectural changes in general;
- flower pots, baskets, urns, rugs and a place to rest on the upper platform;
- and much more.

## World Edits:

Exterior only overhaul of Tel Aruhn, adding new clutter, detailing, flora, and other assets to the exteriors of Tel Aruhn.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Will probably conflict with Tel Aruhn Chronicles and Zafirbel Beacons, but otherwise is compatible with all other Tel Aruhn and Azura's Coast mods.
