Adds a Grand Aqueduct to the Ascadian Isles, just to the north west of Vivec City, as well as various bits of water related infrastructure that brings fresh water to the city's citizens. Also adds a small Tribunal monastery on the edge of the Ascadian Isles, and a new, brief Temple pilgrimage quest.

## Features:

- A Grand Aqueduct that connects Vivec City with a natural hot spring, bringing a source of freshwater to the city
- A small Tribunal Monastery containing the hot spring and a new Temple shrine
- A water treatment facility found in the Foreign Quarter canalworks, that cleans and sanitizes the water before it is sent further along in the city
- A new Temple pilgrimage quest involving the new shrine; To begin you must be a Temple member and speak with Danso Indules at the Vivec Temple; non Temple members may receive the shrine's blessing, but you must have the right offering

## Optional Plugin Features

- a plugin that adds smaller aqueducts that connect the various cantons
- a plugin adding the same sort aqueduct between the Telvanni and Arena cantons; separate for compatibility purposes
- a plugin adding OAAB Data Velothi fountains to the canton plazas

## World Edits:

Adds a new location to the edge of the Ascadian Isles and Bitter Coast, including associated exterior assets and new interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Comes with alternate versions for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Vivec - God the City, and Vivec - City of Swords.

Consequently, should be compatible with most Vivec city overhauls, and is confirmed compatible with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage and Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees.
