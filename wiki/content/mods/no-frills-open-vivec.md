> Opens up Vivec and (optionally) moves the Foreign Quarter canton a bit.

## Description:

**No-Frills Open Vivec (Paper Map Placement)**

This version makes no changes other than opening Vivec's cantons and placing the Foreign Quarter into a symmetrical position more akin to the paper map that shipped with Morrowind. No big changes otherwise, no additional lights, signposts, or similar.

**No-Frills Open Vivec (Vanilla Placement)**

Exact same thing, except the cantons are not moved.

## World Edits

Replaces the Vivec cantons with new open versions with exterior plazas, including associated exterior cell edits. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly incompatible with other Vivec city overhaul mods (at least those that have their own open canton variants) and any mods that add new elements to the Vivec plazas.

Comes with compatibility patches for Uvirith's Legacy and Main Quest Enhancers.
