> Towering over the Grazelands to the south, Arkngchend-Nchumez is inhabited by adventurers and villains trying to uncover its secrets. Inside the ruins is a small group who's been working at crafting and making new types of Dwemer armors and weapons, and may even have insight on what kind of secrets are within.

## Features:

- A short quest involved to find a relic and activate it.
- Compact style Dwemer armor pieces to buy.
- Dwemer "punk" style armor pieces that you can have crafted from specific parts.
- New animated relic staff and Dwemer punk short sword.
- Functional elevator within the ruins.
- A place to park your airship with a nice view.
- Includes a player home or temporary resting place at the top of one of the towers, left behind by the inhabitants within.
- New scripted and animated events.
- Ultimate verticality!

## World Edits

Adds a new location with new associated interiors to the edge of the southern Grazelands. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods. No known conflicts reported.
