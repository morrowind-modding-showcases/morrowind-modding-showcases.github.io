This mod is a small overhaul of Tel Branora, designed to expand on the theme of it being a remote and alien Telvanni settlement, ruled by a mad and powerful sorceress.

## Features:

- Spider tunnels in the tower dungeon.
- An extravagant alchemy shop.
- Expanded docks: Tel Branora originally had just two shacks. Now, there are two more, along with additional fishing-related clutter, giving the docks a more lively and fleshed-out appearance.
- New Telvanni boat model: A new, organic-style boat model by DimNussens replaces the generic Nordic longboat found in vanilla. The boat is adorned with lanterns, fishing gear, ropes, and more, adding to Tel Branora's alien and distinct atmosphere.
- Optional addon with new misc quests: This addon includes quests adapted from the old mod Tales of Tel Branora﻿.

## World Edits:

Main plugin file edits the two main cells of Tel Branora and a landscape edit to cell 14, -13.

Also edits the vanilla interior cells of "Tel Branora, Lower Tower" and "Tel Branora, Tower Dungeon".

Optional Tales of Tel Branora addon edits the following interiors:

- Beran Ancestral Tomb
- Kumarahaz
- Maba-Ilu
- Tel Branora, Sethan's Tradehouse
- Yakanalit

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with Tethered Tel Branora, Tel Branora - Domain of the Mad Mistress, RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse, and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.

A compatibility patch is available for Kumarahaz - The Bandit Lair.
