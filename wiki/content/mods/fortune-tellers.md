> Adds three fortune tellers who will read your future for ten gold, once per day. You get a small blessing from this, and depending on the fortune maybe a temporary skill or attribute fortify effect.
>
> The fortunes and blessings given are based on certain quests that you have yet to start, and are filtered entirely randomly so may be immediately relevant and useful or distant and obscure. There is also a degree of interpretation to their words.

## World Edits

Adds NPCs and minor clutter to three interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with mods that change the layout of the South Wall Cornerclub or The Rat in the Pot. Otherwise should be compatible with most other mods.
