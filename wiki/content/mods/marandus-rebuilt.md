This mod adds a new structures to the Marandus complex, including nearby docks and a tower. NPCs have also been given a facelift with new OAAB integrations. Redoran players can avail of the stronghold garrison's services.

The stronghold is also now the scene for several quests for Hlaalu player characters, as you can now once again take on the role of Felsen Sethandus or Relmerea Saramto to infiltrate Marandus. There are three new quests in the fort for Felsen/Relmerea, as well as a quest to report the happenings of these quests your Hlaalu superiors.

To access the Hlaalu quests, speak to Nileno Dorvayn in Balmora after completing the quest Disguise and holding the helmet you receive from that quest. 

Redoran players can talk to Neminda in Ald'ruhn to start a quest to uncover the fate of Felsen Sethandus or Relmerea Saramto.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of Marandus, as well as places quest NPCs and objects in a variety of other cells.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Incompatible with Better Dunmer Strongholds, but otherwise has no reported mod conflicts.
