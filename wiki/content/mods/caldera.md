Detailing Caldera. Nothing superfluous, just decorations.

## World Edits:

Adds new clutter and detailing to Caldera, along with landscape edits.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Will have conflicts with other Caldera mods, mostly in way of clutter blocking or floating. See [Lucevar's Compatibility Version](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/47224?tab=files) for an option that's compatible with most of the major Caldera addition mods out there.
