ISN is a "vanilla plus" style overhaul for Seyda Neen. It adds more clutter, plant life, and visual storytelling around the village, without transforming Seyda Neen into something it was never meant to be. The overhaul comes with a purchasable player home, and a few secrets as well.

## World Edits:

Primarily an exterior clutter overhaul of Seyda Neen and its environs. Also adds two new interiors, and objects to a number of vanilla interiors in Seyda Neen and surrounding areas.

## Other Notes:

Immersive Seyda Neen has been used as the basis for nearly all modern Seyda Neen overhaul mods, and will, as a result, conflict with those more recent city and town mods.
