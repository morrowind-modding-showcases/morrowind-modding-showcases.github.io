This mod adds a bit of environmental detailing and clutter in and around Tel Branora as well as a bit of clutter to the tower interior itself. Many of the new elements are meant to reflect the mad personality of the tower's mistress. 

## World Edits:

Adds terrain statics and clutter to Tel Branora and the immediate surroundings. Also overhauls parts of the Tel Branora tower, including the cells "Tel Branora, Lower Tower", "Tel Branora, Upper Tower" and "Tel Branora, Upper Tower, Therana's Chambers".

Makes NO Landscape Edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with Azura's Coast Redone, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Tel Branora Expansion, and RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse.

A compatibility patch is available for Tethered Tel Branora.
