## World Edits:

Adds new NPCs riding guars to various locations and roadways. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

A variety of compatibility patches exist for mods that alter the terrain or pathways normally taken by Riders NPCs. Otherwise, it's generally compatible with all other mods.
