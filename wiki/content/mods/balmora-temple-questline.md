> This mod adds a five quest long questline to the Temple in Balmora. The other Temple questlines in base Morrowind are in places where the Temple holds a lot of power, such as Vivec, Ald'ruhn, and Molag Mar. Since these take place in Balmora, they're more about the Temple having to live with and accommodate the Imperials and deal with House Hlaalu. Four out of the five quests have multiple endings.

## World Edits

Adds new quest NPCs and objects to a variety of interior and exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, including most other mods adding quests and/or overhauling the Balmora temple. Confirmed conflict with Mines and Caverns.
