> Meet with Tendndras Lonvyn of the Imperial Legion in Balmora and earn his trust... or enmity.

## Features:

- The character is flexible for role-playing and reacts to your actions.
  ﻿Earning his trust is not easy, but if you succeed, you can benefit from his assistance: receive items, get healed, have your bounty removed, and train.
  ﻿Strengthen your bond by proving yourself experienced, loyal, and giving him time.
  ﻿Or ruin the relationship and make an enemy.
- An optional romance is available, if your relationship develops, but this is not a romance mod. You can reject Dras without missing any content.
  ﻿Accepting his feelings will limit your access to noncommittal flings with other NPCs. However, a skilled "Sneak" may keep the betrayal hidden.
- Dras is also capable of handling the player’s issues with criminal characters, such as Nels Llendo and many others.
  ﻿Despite the minimalist scale, all quests related to crimes include alternative endings, reflecting possible Dras’ intervention in events.
- Additionally, Tendndras may assist you during dangerous quests, though he will return to his post once the task is complete.
  ﻿Dras is immortal while accompanying the player, immune to all diseases, and automatically repairs his own equipment. No tedious micromanagement of potions, scrolls, and similar items.

## World Edits

Minor edits to interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods.
