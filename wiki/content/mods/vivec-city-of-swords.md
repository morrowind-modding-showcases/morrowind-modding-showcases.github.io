> This mod is a complete and total overhaul/upgrade of the city, inside and out. Including a new Foreign Quarter, Libary, Prison Moon, docks, Halls of Justice/Wisdom, canton interiors, and more. Player home also added for purchase.

## Features:

- Bring Vivec's Palace into the middle of the city as a central landmark.
- Consolidate the three main levels of the Foreign Quarter into a single "Grand Plaza".
- Remove two cantons entirely (Delyn and Olms), to make room for an actual city surrounding the palace.
- Not only give the city a more pronounced front entrance, but to give each canton it's own front entrance as well.
- Rebuild the Library of Vivec from scratch (everyone is welcome to submit in-universe books and have them added to the library with credit)
- Placed a major and prominent port at the south end of the city.
- Rebuild the Halls of Justice and Wisdom from scratch.
- Added a Private Quarters for the player available for purchase in the Foreign Quarter.
- Rebuild the Prison Moon Baar Dau in order to: Look better, (including an exclusive new asset for the exterior) have a more fitting and grand interior, accommodate stealth gameplay)

## World Edits

Extensive overhaul of the city of Vivec, including both interiors and exteriors, as well as landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Extremely unlikely to be compatible with anything touching Vivec, most mods will need compatibility patches to work properly with Vivec - City of Swords. Will also conflict with Ministry of Truth overhauls and replacers. Should be compatible with the gate-less version of Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage though.
