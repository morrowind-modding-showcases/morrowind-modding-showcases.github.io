## Description:

A former priory of Talos overlooking the Sea of Ghosts, now taken over by a struggling Imperial Navy garrison.

## Features:
- A former Prior remains to tend the injured and run the infirmary, selling potions.
- One of the garrison has taken it upon himself to set up a bar in the storage area and has a supply of local and foreign drinks.
- The quartermaster has a small surplus of TR Imperial Navy gear for sale.
- Beds are for whoever can claim them, but the Quarters are off limits to any not of an Imperial rank.

## World Edits:

Adds a new location to the northern Sheogorad region with a new structure and associated exterior cluttering and new interior.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, including Spines of Madness.
