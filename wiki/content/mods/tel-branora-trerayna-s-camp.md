A very simple little mod that adds a camp outside Tel Branora for Trerayna Dalen and her followers.

## World Edits:

Modifies two exterior cells in Tel Branora. Includes landscape edits for both 15, -13 and 14, -13.

Makes no interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Tethered Tel Branora, Tel Branora - Domain of the Mad Mistress, Tel Branora Expansion, RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse, Atronach Expansion, and Azura's Coast Redone.

No Known Conflicts have been Reported.
