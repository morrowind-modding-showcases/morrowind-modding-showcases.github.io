> A mix of open and closed cantons and some additional cool features for Vivec:)

## World Edits

Makes exterior and interior edits to a variety of cells in Vivec, but makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with other Vivec city overhauls that open or close various plazas, but theoretically, should be compatible with simple exterior clutter overhauls of the city. Includes compatibility patches for Welcome to the Arena, Guild of Vampire Hunters, and Abot's travel mods.
