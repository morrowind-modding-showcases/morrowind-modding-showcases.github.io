This mod overhauls the entire Foyada Mamaea up until Ghostgate making the area much more interesting and varied. Most changes are visual in nature.

## World Edits:

Landscape overhaul covering the Foyada Mamaea, adding new terrain assets, flora, cluttering, and other detailing. Includes a number of landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with Ghostgate Fortress and Darknut's Greater Dwemer Ruins, but conflicts with Dallara Imperial Forts.

Will presumably have conflicts with any other landscape overhauls touching the same cells with landscape edits.
