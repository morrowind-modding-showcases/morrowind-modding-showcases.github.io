> This mod expands the Death of a Taxman quest in Morrowind. The killer catches wind of your investigation and goes into hiding. Mistakenly he left a note for his love but it ends up leading the player right to him.

## World Edits

Adds a new dungeon to the Bitter Coast with minor exterior clutter. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with other mods changing the Death of a Taxman quest, but otherwise should be compatible with most Seyda Neen mods.
