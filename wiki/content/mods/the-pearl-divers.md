Made for Summer Modjam 2022! Meet One-Eye and Beela Fire-Beard, two Argonian pearl divers with a mysterious connection.

## World Edits:

Adds two small locations, one in the Norvayn Bay, near Vivec, and one in the Sheogorad, northeast of Ald Redaynia. Both contain a handful of new clutter and assets to exterior cells, but otherwise contain NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, no known conflicts reported.
