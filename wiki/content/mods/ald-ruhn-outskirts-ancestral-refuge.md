> Adds a cozy old temple to the hill above Ald'Ruhn. Now it serves as a base for the Ancestral mages. Also you can eat and rest there during your pilgrimage.

## World Edits

Adds two new locations to the Ashlands near Ald'ruhn, including exterior clutter and detailing. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most other nearby mods. A [compatibility patch is available here](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59785) for City of Ash. Has a confirmed conflict with Little Landscapes - Nix Hound Hunting Grounds.
