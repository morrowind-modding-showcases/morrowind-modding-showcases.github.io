> Expands Balmora into an actual city with an added; 69 buildings, 77 interior cells and 300+ unique NPC's alongside a Player Home, Quests, Factions, Taverns, and more!Hla Oad has also been expanded, since the large ships dock there and the smaller rowboats and skifs take the cargo into Balmora, the two settlements are linked together.

## World Edits

Massive city expansion for Balmora and Hla Oad, impacting a wide range of exterior cells, including lots of new buildings, clutter, detailing, associated interiors, and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Highly incompatible with most other mods expanding or overhauling Balmora and Hla Oad. A number of compatibility patches do exist though, including for Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Stoneflower House, Bell Towers of Vvardenfell, Morrowind Crafting, and Compatible Odai River Upper Overhaul.

Will likely conflict with most other mods in these areas.
