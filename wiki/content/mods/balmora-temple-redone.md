> A unique temple design for Balmora based on the Dibellan temple in Tamriel Rebuilts' city of Ud Hleryn.
>
> The overall theme is a pairing of Hlaalu and Velothi, with the more mercantile services sat alongside the painted murals and the raised dais where the Master of the Temple performs her sermons.
>
> The interior main hall is now spacious and open with dedicated space for the alchemy and scroll sellers. The new Tower of Respite houses the Temple kitchen and Masters quarters, with the Tower of Illumination taking the place of the old catacombs.

## World Edits

Overhauls and replaces the interior and exterior of the Balmora temple. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Includes compatibility patches for Temple Throat Singers and Animated Morrowind. Also includes a compatibility version with access to the original Balmora interior. As such, should be compatible with most mods that don't overhaul the exterior of the Balmora temple. Confirmed conflict with Amazing Balmora Expansion.
