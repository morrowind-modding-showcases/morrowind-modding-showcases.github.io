Adds a second floor and a questline to the East Empire Company Hall in Ebonheart. Also adds a side wing to [[Mzahnch]] dwemer ruin.

## Overview

This mod adds a second floor office and a questline to the East Empire Company Hall in Ebonheart. This mod has eight quests that are mostly speechcraft-based. If you perform well and make the best business decisions, you may be rewarded with a (very nice) office that could work as a small player home at the Company Hall.

## The Questline

The recently-demoted Deputy Laro Berenius is undergoing a performance review. He asks the player to help him improve the Vvardenfell branch of the East Empire Company's profits so he can be restored to his former position. Tasks assigned include negotiating with a member of the Caldera Mining Company, investigating fraud at a glass mine, and looking into Dwemer smugglers in the Ascadian Isles. The choices you make matter and can change things in future quests. The most dangerous enemies you will face in these quests are centurion spiders and a few blighted nix-hounds. They can easily be completed by a low-level player.

## World Edits:

Minor NPC and quest item additions to various interior and exterior cells. Adds an expansion to Mzahnch and the East Empire Company.

## Other Notes:

Should be compatible with most mods, may have conflicts with major Ebonheart overhauls (like Morrowind Rebirth), but otherwise doesn't have any known conflicts.
