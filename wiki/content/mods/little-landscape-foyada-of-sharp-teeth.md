Overhauls the northernmost part of Foyada Bani-Dad, near the Derelict Shipwreck near Khuul.

## World Edits:

Adds exterior terrain and flora assets to the foyada dividing the West Gash from the northern Ashlands. Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.


## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with OAAB Shipwrecks and Rocky West Gash. Probably also compatible with Dwemer Legacy Reforged and Markgran Forest. Otherwise, unlikely to conflict with any other mods.
