## Description:

In the City of Ald'rhun, right next to the Mages guild, lives a retired Buoyant Armiger. He has taken up mineral collecting in his spare time. Recently however, he has had a difficult time getting some more rare and unique minerals. That is where you come in.

This mod adds a small quest as well as three additional side objectives that you can complete for extra rewards. You can complete them even after the main part of the quest is finished. So you don't need to hold off on finishing the mod.

Also included are improvements of two dungeons. These are the Eluba-Addon egg mine and the Grotto of the same name. There aren't that many mods that touch these two locations to my knowledge so you should be fine with compatibility.

## World Edits:

Adds a new residence with associated statics and exterior clutter, plus new interior, to Ald'ruhn.

Also includes an overhaul of the Eluba-Addon Egg Mine and Eluba-Addon Grotto.

## Other Notes:

Includes a compatibility patch for City of Ash. Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.

Unknown compatibility with other Ald'ruhn city overhauls.
