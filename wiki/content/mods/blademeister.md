> Tucked away in the flooded back tunnels of Addamasartus is a talking, shapeshifting blade that hungers for the power it once held. It asks that you help it regain what was lost, and in exchange, it will be your most powerful ally.

## Features:

- Felthorn is a versatile shapeshifting companion that can take on the form of any weapon type or any Daedra, if you provide it with the means to do so. Feeding Felthorn a soul gem filled with Daedra soul will allow it to add that creature's form to its repertoire, and locating lost weapon fragments will similarly unlock the corresponding weapon types.
- Felthorn will slowly grow in power, based on the player's level and offerings of gems, ores, and Daedra hearts. Once fully realized, it will be on par with many artifacts, and many times more adaptable.
- In creature form, Felthorn functions as a fully capable (if lightweight) companion. Some compromises were made due to its unusual nature, a lack of Companion Share being a big example.
- The -Felthorn- spell lets you speak to Felthorn if it is both in weapon form and on your person, and teleports Felthorn to you while in creature form.
- If you follow Felthorn's quest all the way to the end, you will unlock two additional creature transformations apart from its basic lineup.

## World Edits

Adds quest-related objects to a variety of exterior and interior locations. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Adds objects to a variety of dungeon interior cells which may cause conflicts with various dungeon overhauls. Compatibility patches exist for Mines and Caverns, Adanumuran Reclaimed, Vennin's Addamasartus Overhaul, and Daedric Shrine Overhaul FULL.
