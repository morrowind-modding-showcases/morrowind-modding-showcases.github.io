> This carefully crafted mod introduces an Imperial lighthouse that stands tall and proud in the heart of Ebonheart's harbor.
>
> Immerse yourself in the bustling life of the harbor as the newly added lighthouse illuminates the waterfront with its steady beacon. Towering above the quays and fishing boats, this addition not only enhances the visual charm of Ebonheart but also serves as a functional navigational aid for sailors and adventurers alike.
> At the heart of this mod is Emelia Caro, an Imperial woman who takes on the role of the lighthouse keeper.

## Features

- Adds an unique lighthouse to the harbor of Ebonheart.
- Detailed cluttering with dedicated meshes and meshes from OAAB_data and Tamriel_Data.
- Adds one NPC in the role of lighthouse keeper.

## World Edits

Adds a new building with associated interior cell to either central Ebonheart or just south of Ebonheart, depending on version used. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Depending on version, highly compatible with other mods overhauling or expanding Ebonheart.
