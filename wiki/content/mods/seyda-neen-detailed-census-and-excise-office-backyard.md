What is arguably the most visited location in Morrowind is also the least interesting to look at, this little mod aims to make it look and feel more lived in.

## World Edits:

Clutter and detail overhaul of the exterior backyard of the Census and Excise Office.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Will have mixed compatibility with other Seyda Neen city overhauls, depending on their level of clutter in the small area that this mod covers. Some mods do provide their own compatibility patches.

A patch for [Ashfront - Bitter Coast can be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59601)
