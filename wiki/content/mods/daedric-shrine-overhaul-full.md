A complete/merged version of the Daedric Shrine Overhaul mods.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of all Daedric shrines on Vvardenfell. Primarily includes new clutter, statics, creatures and NPCs.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

This is a compilation of the following mods:
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Vaermina
- ﻿Daedric Shrine Overhaul Exterior Sheogorath﻿
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Exteriors Malacath﻿
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Exteriors Mehrunes Dagon and Molag Bal
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Sheogorath﻿
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Malacath
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Mehrunes Dagon
- Daedric Shrine Overhaul Molag Bal﻿

Alternative version, featuring bug fixes and patches, [can be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/58966)

An [add-on for this mod](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/58725) is available that expands the shrine to Vaermina.

Will conflict with other Daedric shrine overhaul mods.
