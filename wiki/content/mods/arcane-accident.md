> This mod has been uploaded for ModJam2026. Submitted for the theme of "Arcane Arts". A Breton has moved into the Vivec's Foreign Quarter (lower waistworks). He has been such a quiet resident until today when loud crashes could be heard coming from his quarters...for a brief period of time.

## World Edits

Adds a new interior connected to the Vivec Foreign Quarter Lower Waistworks. Makes NO landscape edits and NO exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with mods changing the layout of the Foreign Quarter Waistworks.  A[patch is available for BCOM here](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/60085). Will conflict with Imperial Employment Office.
