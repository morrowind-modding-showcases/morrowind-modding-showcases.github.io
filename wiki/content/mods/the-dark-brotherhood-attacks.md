## Description:

This adds a new quest arc to Tribunal that replaces the randomized Dark Brotherhood attacks. Now the Dark Brotherhood have a very well thought-out plan that will take you into the depths of the completely overhauled Arys Ancestral Tomb. By the end of this new quest, you'll be quite angry and feel compelled to chase the Dark Brotherhood back to Mournhold and confront them for revenge.

## Features
- A new quest arc.
- A better start to Tribunal
- A personality for Dandras Vules 
- Development for Apelles Mutius
- An overhauled Arys Ancestral Tomb
- & Stays lore-friendly to the expansion

## World Edits:

Overhauls and replaces the Arys Ancestral Tomb with new interior expansions. Impacts a small number of other locations with quest objects and/or NPCs.
