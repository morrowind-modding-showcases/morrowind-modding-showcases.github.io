> The search for a missing apprentice turns into a sadistic, maddening trial. Escape the grip of your demented captor and make a new friend in the process. If you escape with your lives, that is. Part of Summer Modjam 2023.

## World Edits

Adds a portal to the top of a tower in Balmora, in addition to a new NPC in the Balmora, Guild of Mages. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, including most Balmora city expansions and overhauls.
