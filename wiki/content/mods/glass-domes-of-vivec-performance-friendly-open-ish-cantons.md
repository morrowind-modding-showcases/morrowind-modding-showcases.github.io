> Replaces the tops of Vivec's cantons with "glass" domes, so that the plaza interiors are gloriously lit by daylight and aren't as miserable. They will react to weather and night time, also.
>
> Inspired by open Vivec canton mods like No-Frills Open Vivec, but without the performance drop of actually moving all those NPCs into the exterior world.

## World Edits

Replaces the exterior models of the Vivec cantons with new versions, including a handful of exterior references, as well as their interior countparts. Makes NO landscape edits

## Other Notes

Will conflict with any mods that move the cantons or otherwise open up the canton plazas. Also, notably, this mod is considered potentially obsolete, and newer alternatives, like [Moonrain Edition](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/48946) and [Mixed Cantons of Vivec](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49037) are recommended instead.
