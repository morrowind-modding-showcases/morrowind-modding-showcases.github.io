> This mod seeks to make Gnaar Mok feel more like the impoverished but active fishing village it was originally intended to be. BCOMpatible version available.

## Features:

- 4 new inhabited shacks
- A fish seller
- Dreugh Jigger's Rest is now a functioning tavern (hammocks moved to a basement cave)
- Enhanced and updated exteriors and interiors
- Does not touch the Arenim Estate (yet)

## World Edits

Interior and exterior overhaul of Gnaar Mok, adding new clutter, detailing, and new buildings with associated interiors to Gnaar Mok. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Comes with a compatibility patch for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Should be compatible with most other minor Gnaar Mok mods, but will presumably conflict with bigger Gnaar Mok overhauls and expansions.

Confirmed compatible with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, Verdant Bitter Coast, Shipyards of Vvardenfell, Vality's Bitter Coast, and Shabby Gnaar Mok.
