> Adds new ports to the beautiful city of Vivec

## World Edits

Expands the city of Vivec with new ports and harbors attached to the St. Olms and St. Delyn cantons. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most Vivec city overhauls and expansions, but will conflict with both Rethinking Vivec (which the ports are actually originally from) and Vivec - God the City.
