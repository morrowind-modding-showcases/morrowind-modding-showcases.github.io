Kilcunda's Balmora is a relatively simple lore-friendly Balmora overhaul: Rather than adding entire new buildings/districts/etc this mod expands on the vanilla Balmora layout in an attempt to make it more detailed and believable.

The mod also adds a new-look canal, more detailed surrounding areas and entrances, increased lighting, three new NPC's and more! Now Balmora feels more like the Hlaalu trading capital of Vvardenfel as described in-game.

## World Edits:

Exterior only overhaul of Balmora, includes new terrain and city assets, as well as new clutter and detailing. Makes landscape edits to many of the cells in and around Balmora.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

A more compatible version of [Kilcunda's Balmora can be found here](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/57685), making it compatible with some other popular smaller Balmora mods.

Otherwise, will conflict with most other Balmora city overhaul mods.
