> A dark adventure featuring a new island and dungeon, new items and enemies!

## World Edits

Adds a new landmass north of the Northern Ashlands and west of the Sheogorad, including landscape edits, exterior detailing, and associated new interiors. Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with other landmass mods in the same area, and has confirmed conflicts with Yul Marshee and the Visage of Mzund and Drenim Ancestral Tomb.

Confirmed compatible with Tomb of the Snow Prince, Spines of Madness, Sheogorad - Groves of the North, and Baal Duun.
