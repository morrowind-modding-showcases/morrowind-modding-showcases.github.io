> MNC: Ebonheart extends the castle Ebonheart and its surrounding area with several buildings and NPCs with their own dialogue.

## World Edits

Extensive landscape edits around Ebonheart, with numerous new buildings and associated interiors.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with most other mods in and around the fortress of Ebonheart. A bug fix for the water in MNC: Ebonheart [can be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/43354)
