> Embark on a journey to the cursed isle of Baal Duun in a quest to uncover the secrets hidden within the depths of a mythical daedric island. On the road between Gnisis and Ald Velothi,  you may run into a naive yet intrepid archeologist who is in possession of a mysterious key. Will you assist him in discovering what it unlocks?

## World Edits

Adds a new island north of Vvardenfell.

## Features

- A new landmass
- A quest designed by the renowned Melchior Dhark
- Expansive dungeons designed by Seelof and myself
- The possibility of unlocking a new travel network
