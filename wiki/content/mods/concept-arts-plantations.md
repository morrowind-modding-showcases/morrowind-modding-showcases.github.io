A concept arts-based overhaul of the Arvel and Dren plantations.

## World Edits:

Overhauls some of the interiors and exteriors of the Arvel and Dren Plantations, including new flora and building assets, as well as landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Will conflict with any other plantation overhaul of the Arvel and Dren plantations. Has some minor clipping with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage.
