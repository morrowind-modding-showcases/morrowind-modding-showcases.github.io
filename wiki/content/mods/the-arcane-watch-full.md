## Description:

The Order of the Arcane Watch is an organization of unlikely alliances, tenuous relationships, and opposing motivations, held together by just one interest: aberrant magic.

Some are trying to righteously protect Tamriel. Some are chasing their own interests. And others may just be trying to eliminate their competition.

Regardless of your motives and background, if you have an interest in slaying cultists, stopping the daedra, or putting the undead back to rest, the Order of the Arcane Watch has a place for you.

## Features﻿
- A lore-friendly, RP-friendly reason to explore the TR Mainland if you want, without relying on TR_Mainland.esm!
- A lore-friendly, RP-friendly reason to explore other quest mods! (other quest mods not necessary)
- New NPCs!
- New dungeons! Delve beneath a lighthouse, into a mad flesh mage's dungeon, into a mysterious Oblivion realm, and more!
- Several hours of gameplay
- A quest written by Hyojason! :)
- Seven new quests in total!

## World Edits:

Quest mod adding new locations, NPCs, and objects to a variety of exterior and interior cells across Vvardenfell. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

A [Beautiful Cities of Morrowind patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/55563) is available for compatibility.

Otherwise, will conflict with Vivec - God the City and any mods that significantly alter the Vivec, Foreign Quarter Waistworks.
