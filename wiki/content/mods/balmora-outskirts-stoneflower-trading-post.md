This mod adds a new cozy trading post/tavern to the outskirts of Balmora. There you can meet a Khajiit bard who sings her native songs, try delicious bantam guar meat, and explore the nearby egg mine...

## Features:
- Adds a great place to relax and sell goods on your way to Balmora, Stoneflower trading post enhances the landscape, plus there's a simple quest that lets you explore a new egg mine nearby;
- Unique Khajiit bard Possya who's inspired by my cat (Ruffin Vangarr created her head based on the photo below, thank you very much!);
- New awesome helmet by Ruffin Vangarr - Imperial Dust Helm + new cool amulet;
- You can't rent a bed at the Stoneflower trading post, because the rooms are constantly occupied by the merchants, but in case you wanna stay there, the owners can offer you to buy a room downstairs, it costs 2000 gold.
- Version 1.7 adds a new dungeon to explore.

## World Edits:

Adds a new tavern with exterior cluttering and detailing on the outskirts of Balmora, with new dungeon entrances in a couple of nearby cells.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with The Stonewood Pass Reworked, Devilish Rocky West Gash, The Beacons of Mamaea, Moonlight on the Odai River, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, and most Balmora city overhaul mods.

A compatibility patch is available for [Illuminated Order here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/54814)

Will presumably conflict with the primary location for Nerevar's House Mod.
