A small and very cute replacer of the magical Tel Branora.

## World Edits:

Clutters and details the exterior cells of Tel Branora.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

While not confirmed, will likely be incompatible with any other Tel Branora overhaul mods.

Should be compatible with RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse and Azura's Coast Redone.
