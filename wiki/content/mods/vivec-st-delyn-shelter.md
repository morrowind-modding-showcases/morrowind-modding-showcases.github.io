> This mod improves the Vivec, St. Delyn Waist North-Two cell to make it a better player home. It also adds a separate bedroom cell off the main room.

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of St. Delyn Waist North-Two. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with other mods overhauling or touching St. Delyn Waist North-Two (there's a few), but should be compatible with all other mods.
