Explore the sunken and lost shrine of boethiah with the exterior getting a face lift and feeling like its been claimed by the sea along with an underwater daedric shrine interior to explore and rob the shrine of its riches.

## Features:
-Overhauled the exterior off the sunken ruins.
-Made a new underwater shrine interior.
-Fixed afew floating bethesda rocks.
-Defined and detailed the sea floor making the ruins stand out more. 
-Added loot in the shrine.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the exteriors of the Shrine of Boethiah, adding new objects, detailing, clutter and landscape edits. Also adds a new interior dungeon.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with most other mods, except those editing the Shrine of Boethiah.
