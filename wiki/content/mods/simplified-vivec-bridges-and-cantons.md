> Simplifies navigation around Vivec by removing unnecessary bridges and Waistworks corridors, and connecting up the Waistworks directly with the Plazas.

## Features:

- Removed unnecessary bridges between cantons - there is now exactly one bridge between each pair, most of which can be accessed from both the Waistworks and Canalside levels. (Exceptions - Foreign Quarter and Temple Canton bridges are Canalside only for obvious reasons; the bridge between Telvanni Compound and the Arena is Waistworks only as described below.)
- Moved the entire Telvanni Compound 256 units to the east, and removed the lower connecting bridge to Arena. Now there is a theoretically navigable waterway for any ships at North Landing to actually leave the docks and reach the sea.
- Removed unnecessary passageways and entrances from the Waistworks - all Waistworks levels now have exactly and only two exterior entrances, thereby removing the four extremely long passageways that only served to go back outside.
- Added a connecting passageway from the Waistworks to the Plaza - every canton's Waistworks now has one direct passage to the Plaza without needing to go outside (except Arena, where that didn't really make sense).
- Added a teleporter entrance to the Foreign Quarter Canalworks from the exterior Foreign Quarter Canalside, so you can get to the stores and Imperial Cult Shrine faster.
- Added signage to each of the passageways between levels and outside main entrances so you know which level of the canton you're on.
- Hlaalu, Redoran and Telvanni Compound signs now say "Compound" rather than "Canton", reflecting the canton names used in dialogue.
- Fixed the illogical order of some of St. Olms and St. Delyn's Canalside and Waistworks North and South houses - they are now all One - Two - Three in order from west to east.

## World Edits

Edits the interiors and exteriors of the cantons of Vivec, including moving and adding of canton structures. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Likely incompatible with most other Vivec city overhaul mods.
