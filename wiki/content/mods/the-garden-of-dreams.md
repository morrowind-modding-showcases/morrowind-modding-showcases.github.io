> Witness the mythical interplay of death and fate manifest across surreal realms as you follow the path of a doomed individual who is determined to escape their fate.

## Features:

- Follow the horrifying path of an individual cursed by prophecy to die in every possible outcome.
- Catch glimpses of a mysterious presence hunting you across magical realms.
- Unravel the mythical nature of how death and fate are intertwined in The Elder Scrolls universe.
- Three Morag Tong writ quests from the new Monastery of Mephala.
- Almost 15,000 objects using over 500 custom models placed in expansive new areas to explore.
- Voice acting by [Renara Hawke](https://renara-hawke-va.co.uk/).

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Azura's Coast, as well as quest-related objects and NPCs to a variety of interior and exterior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

A compatibility patch for Tel Raloran is available in the mod download. Otherwise, has no known reported conflicts.
