Help a dunmer to get some money for his travels by buying his shack in the Bitter Coast. But why is he in such a hurry to get rid of it?

Search for him on the road between Seyda Neen and Ebonheart.

## World Edits:

Exterior only additions to the Bitter Coast south of Seyda Neen, featuring a new player home location with a new interior.

Otherwise makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.
