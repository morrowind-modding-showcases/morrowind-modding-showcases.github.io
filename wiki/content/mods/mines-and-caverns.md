This mod improves several vanilla dungeons aesthetically and/or expands them.

## World Edits:

Large cavern overhaul mod covering most of the caverns and mines on Vvardenfell, providing expanded interiors and new additions.

Also edits the exteriors of several caverns, mostly for the sake of moving door markers.

## Other Notes:

Incompatible with most other cavern overhaul mods.
