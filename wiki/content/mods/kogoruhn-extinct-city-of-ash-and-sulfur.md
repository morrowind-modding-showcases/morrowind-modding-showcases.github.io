While wandering through the desolated landscapes of northern Ashlands, Nerevarine comes across the old capital city of Chimer tribe - once known as Kogoruhn. This ancient place, half buried beneath the ash and sulfur is now home of horror which never sleeps... The Sixth House. Discover this terrifying place hidden under the shadow of Red Mountain.

## World Edits:

Complete and extensive edits to both the interiors and exteriors of Kogoruhn, including overhauls of every interior of the Dunmer stronghold, and notable landscape edits to the exterior.
