## Overview:

This mod adds a Chapel of Arkay to the outskirts of Pelagiad. It is meant to serve as resting place for Western colonists in Vvardenfell, fallen legionnaires, and Dunmer converts to the Imperial Cult. The chapel is complete with a small exterior graveyard, and an Imperial crypt may be found beneath.

This mod also contains a brief quest to inter the remains of a fallen legionnaire at the Chapel of Arkay. 

## World Edits:

Adds a new location to the edge of Pelagiad, with some exterior clutter and detailing and associated new interior.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Should be compatible with most other Pelagiad city and town overhauls, as well as Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage. No conflicts reported.
