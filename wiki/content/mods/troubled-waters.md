Dive for secrets and uncover the mysteries of the Cliffracer's Roost in this short adventure.

## World Edits:

Adds new quest NPCs to the village of Khuul, and a new exterior location in the seas of the Sheogorad, just north of the Ashlands coastline and south of Ald Redaynia.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, no known conflicts reported.
