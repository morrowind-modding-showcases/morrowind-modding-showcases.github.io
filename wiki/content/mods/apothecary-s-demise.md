> Apothecary's Demise is a quest mod (For any level, +11 for dungeon) about choice, that starts in the quaint abode in the Ascadian Isles. Upon entering this nice Apothecary's Abode, you are greeted with a dead apothecary, and a note written in his dying breath. The note reads: "Don't go in the cellar, under any circumstance!"

## Features:
- A quest featuring choice
- A player home with new features as quest progresses
- New large dungeon, with lots of loot 
- A unique "valdir"- like weapon
- More!

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Ascadian Isles, with associated exterior clutter and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with some Ascadian Isles landscape overhauls, and possibly a few house mods, but otherwise should be compatible with most mods in the Ascadian Isles region.
