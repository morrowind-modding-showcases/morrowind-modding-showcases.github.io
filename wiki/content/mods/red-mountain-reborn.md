Morrowind Rebirth's Red Mountain comes to "Vanilla" + compatibility with Darknut's Greater Dwemer Ruins (& a patch for HOTV's Tomb of the Snow Prince so you can use all 3!), Resdayn Revival's Ghostgate Fortress, Rem's Foyada Mamaea Overhaul, Team Drama Kwama's Kogoruhn Expanded, and Illuminated Order 2.0 Lore Friendly!

## World Edits:

Large landscape overhaul covering the entire Red Mountain region with landscape edits and terrain assets.

## Other Notes:

Comes with compatibility patches for Darknut's Greater Dwemer Ruins, RR - Ghostgate Fortress, OAAB Foyada Mamaea, Kogoruhn Expanded, and The Illuminated Order.

Additional compatibility patches exist for Memento Mori, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Maar Gan - Town of Pilgrimage, Markgran Forest, and Devilish Rocky Red Mountain.

Otherwise, will conflict with most mods in and around Red Mountain.
