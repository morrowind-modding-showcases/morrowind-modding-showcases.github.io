> Explore new Daedric ruins to the east of Urshilaku camp and craft a powerful set of armor with an unusual origin.

## Features:

- New armor in Daedric Akaviri style
- New dungeon with a quest
- Part of the dungeon (Halls of Ash) can become player's home after you cleared it out. There's plenty of storage and a Sigilstone that can teleport you back there.
- Very compatible, no landscape edits.

## World Edits

Adds a new dungeon location to the shores of the Northern Ashlands, with associated exterior clutter and detailing. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with nearly everything, no known conflicts have been reported.
