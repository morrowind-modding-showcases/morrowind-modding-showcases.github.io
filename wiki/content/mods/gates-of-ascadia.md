Fortifies Vivec City and the Ascadian Isles with Velothi gates, walls, and bridges, as well as a handful of holy sites and various other bits of infrastructure.

## World Edits:

Adds new structures in the exterior cells surrounding Vivec, particularly along roadways. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and most Vivec city overhauls. Will conflict with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage (which this mod is based on).
