> The Sixth House have been defeated and Morrowind is saved. Yet, reports of a group of Ashlanders camped outside of Kogoruhn will lead the Nerevarine into a new story. Restore a fallen house, defeat a new enemy, and recover ancient artifacts.

## Features:

- Kick-off a brand new questline.
- Explore a redesigned Mamshar-Disamus Camp.
- Acquire new artifacts, books, and souvenirs.
- Meet several new characters in your journey.
- Help the Mamshar-Disamus continue their ancestors' legacy.
- Face off against Alvath Valen of the Ald-Kena. (Crown Edition Exclusive)
- Full compatibility with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind﻿ (Crown Edition Exclusive)
- Optional integration with The Rise of the Tribe Unmourned (Crown Edition Exclusive)

## World Edits

Adds a new Ashlander camp to the Northern Ashlands, with associated buildings and clutter. Includes new quest objects and NPCs added to a number of vanilla locations. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Merged into Morrowind Extended Cut. May have some minor conflicts with mods overhauling the area around Kogoruhn. Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
