A simple mod that adds a few details to the monastery island.

## World Edits:

Adds new detailing and clutter to the island of the Holamayan Monastery, including new terrain and flora assets. Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with RR Mod Series - Holamayan Monastery Replacer and Azura's Coast Redone. No known conflicts reported.
