> *Just teleported in by the Mages Guild and now don’t want to run down and up the canton ramps or jump off the canton at a great height? Then this mod is for you. *
>
> A small mod that adds a bridge to the Foreign Quarter, crossing over to the upper level of the Redoran Canton for easy access to the Plazas of Vivec.

## World Edits

Adds a new structure spanning the gap between the Foreign Quarter Canton and the Redoran Canton, with associated exterior clutter. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods that don't move the cantons. Worth noting, this mod has been merged into other Vivec city overhauls.
