> This mod adds a new a new set of armor with 2 variants for helm and chest with 2 new hammers.
> You can find the dead paladins in Ularradallaku and Zergonipal daedric shrines.

## World Edits

Adds two dead NPCs to two Daedric shrines. Otherwise makes NO edits to exterior cells and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods.
