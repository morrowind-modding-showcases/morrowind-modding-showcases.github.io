This mod is a major landscape overhaul for the southwestern Grazelands, covering approximately 25% of the whole region. It is now a land of rising flat plateaus, rocky vales, and hackle-lo groves. Rising above it all is the forested hill of the Mora Guradan and its Ald Sunna Vosya: the Sacred Tree of Life.

Confirmed Compatible with OAAB Grazelands, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, The Ashlanders, Thickle-Lo Grove, Halls Of Colossus and The Vanishing Ash-Chirps.
