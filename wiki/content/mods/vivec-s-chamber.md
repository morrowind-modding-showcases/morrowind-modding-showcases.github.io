> This mod adds a small living area in the Palace of Vivec along with some loot for the adventurous.

## World Edits

Adds a new interior cell connected to the Palace of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most other mods that don't heavily change the interior layout of the Palace of Vivec. Comes with a compatibility patch for Versus Vivec.
