Vanilla friendly Pelagiad replacer.
Pelagiad is a cozy, provincial town that emerged near the fort of the same name. It remained so, but additional detail and a couple of new residents were added to the buildings, fort, streets and landscape.

## Features:
- Increased detail of the city, streets and buildings;
- Added owners of containers on the streets of the city;
- Added new trade and citizen.

## World Edits:

Modifies the layout of Pelagiad, adding new clutter, detailing, and buildings with new associated interiors. Includes landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Will likely conflict with most other Pelagiad city overhauls, but it's lightweight enough that it should be compatible with most minor Pelagiad city additions.
