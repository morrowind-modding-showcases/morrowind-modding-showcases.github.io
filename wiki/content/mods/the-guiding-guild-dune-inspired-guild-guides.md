A lore and visual overhaul of Morrowind's Guild Guides. Adds a new faction headquarters for the Guild Guides beneath the Mournhold Royal Palace, as well as a short questline that begins on Vvardenfell and eventually ties in to the main quest of Tribunal.

## World Edits:

Includes minor interior edits to all of the Mages Guild branches on Vvardenfell, as well as exterior static placements in the southern Azura's Coast.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Includes compatibility patches for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and The Wolverine Hall.

Confirmed Compatible with Azura's Coast Redone and Mid Mushroom Overhaul.

No Known Conflicts have been Reported.
