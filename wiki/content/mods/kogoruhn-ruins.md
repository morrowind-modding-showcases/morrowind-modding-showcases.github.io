## World Edits:

Exterior only overhaul of Kogoruhn, adding new statics, clutter, and detail, along with landscape edits.

Makes NO interior cell edits!

## Other Notes:

Compatible with most other mods in the Northern Ashlands, but will conflict with any other Kogoruhn overhaul mods.
