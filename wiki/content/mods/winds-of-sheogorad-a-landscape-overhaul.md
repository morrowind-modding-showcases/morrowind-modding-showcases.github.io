Experience an alpine Sheogorad with icebergs, pines, nordic ruins, and new locations to explore!

## World Edits:

Massive landscape overhaul of the Sheogorad region, covering nearly the entire region with landscape edits, new flora, and other exterior detailing.

Includes three new locations and dungeons.

Also edits a number of vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Winds of Sheogorad has been used as a basis or inspiration for a number of other Sheogorad mods, including Sheogorad - Groves of the North. It is highly incompatible with other mods in this region.
