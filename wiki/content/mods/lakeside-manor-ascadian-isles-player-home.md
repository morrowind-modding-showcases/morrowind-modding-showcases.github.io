>Adds a large player home to the peninsula on Lake Amaya and a lore-friendly way to purchase it.
## World Edits
Edits 4 exterior cells in the Ascadian Isles Region that cover the manor's location by making landscape edits and adding and removing assets. The mod also edits the interior cell "Pelagiad, Halfway Tavern" by adding a custom NPC (Tiana Corelius).
## Other Notes
The mod includes 4 interior cells for the manor which are currently not included in this article.
