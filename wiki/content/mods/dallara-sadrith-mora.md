This Mod modifies the town of Sadrith Mora by adding fortifications and a few buildings.

## World Edits:

Adds new structures and associated clutter and new interiors around the city of Sadrith Mora.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Comes with a compatibility patch for Sadrith Mora - Seat of Power. Will conflict with other walled and closed city of Sadrith Mora mods, but should be compatible with most other mods based in Sadrith Mora, particularly if using the Devilish version.
