> This mod adds more guards to the city of Vivec.

## World Edits

Adds new NPCs to the cantons of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Compatible with all other mods that don't move the cantons of Vivec. Will presumably have mild conflicts with mods that move or replace the Vivec cantons.
