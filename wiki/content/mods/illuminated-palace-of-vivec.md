> This mod adds a lot of candles near the shrines and at the palace steps, left by pilgrims, as well as few flowers and petals near the shrines.

## World Edits

Exterior clutter overhaul of the Palace of Vivec, adding significant light sources and decorations. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods that don't touch the Vivec Palace canton.

Note, a newer version of this mod [can be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/48681)
