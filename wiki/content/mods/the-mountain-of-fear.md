This mod touches up Mount Kand, to make it slightly more worthy of its secondary name: "Mountain of Fear".

## World Edits:

Adds terrain assets and detailing to Mount Kand and the surrounding exterior cells in the Molag Amur Region. Also overhauls the Mount Kand Caverns.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, has no reported mod conflicts (except for those involving the Mount Kand Caverns)
