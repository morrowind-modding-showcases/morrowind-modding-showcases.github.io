This mod touches up Mount Kand, to make it slightly more worthy of its secondary name: "Mountain of Fear".

## Description:

In a one-off line in a minor side quest of the Imperial Cult, Mount Kand is referred to as the "Mountain of Fear." There are two problems with this. First, there is hardly anything scary about Mount Kand at all. Second, Mount Kand is barely a hill, yet alone a mountain.

This mod seeks to rectify this by giving Mount Kand a far more striking, imposing, and mountainous appearance. 

It also adds some minor detailing on the main path up to the mountain's summit. Lanterns and religious decorations now lead the way from Molag Mar for pilgrims seeking to complete the Pilgrimage of Mount Kand.

## World Edits:

Adds new terrain assets and detailing to the area in and around Mount Kand, including an overhaul of the Mount Kand Caverns.

Makes NO landscape edits!

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods and confirmed compatible with Molag Amur Mountains, The Great Scathes, and the vast majority of Molag Mar overhauls.
