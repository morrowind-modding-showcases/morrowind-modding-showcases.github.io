This mod adds hundreds of fern trees (using the trees from Ashfront - Bitter Coast) to the Bitter Coast.

## World Edits:

Primarily adds new tree assets across most of the Bitter Coast region.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with the following mods:
- More Alien Bitter Coast
- OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat
- Shipyards of Vvardenfell (Sadrith Mora - Seyda Neen - Gnaar Mok)
- New Gnaar Mok and Newer Gnaar Mok
- Little Landscapes- Bitter Coast Waterway
- Odai - Lifeblood of Balmora

Likely compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.

Unknown compatibility with Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees.
