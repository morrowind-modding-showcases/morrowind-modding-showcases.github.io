A small mod that adds a bridge to the Dummer stronghold of Marandus, crossing the Nabia River where it meets Lake Nabia.

## World Edits:

Adds statics to two exterior cells.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, has no known mod conflicts.
