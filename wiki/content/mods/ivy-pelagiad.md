> This mod replaces the buildings (common and Nord) in Pelagiad with a different texture, giving Pelagiad a slightly more vibrant look, befitting the Ascadian Isles.

## World Edits

Replaces the buildings of Pelagiad with new models. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Includes patches for Magical Missions, Minuet Cottage, Yul Marshee, Bath Shoppe, Pelagiad Greenhouse, and Apothecary's Demise. Will conflict with mods that change the placement of buildings in Pelagiad.
