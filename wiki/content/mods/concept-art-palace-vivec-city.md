> The Concept Art Palace mod (C.A.P.) aims to bring the Vivec palace closer to Michael Kirkbride's original concept art, restoring its divine grandeur. This mod transforms the palace into a structure worthy of divine presence.

## World Edits

Overhauls the exterior and optionally the interior of the Palace of Vivec, with new clutter, assets, and details. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with any and all mods that don't move or otherwise overhaul the exterior of the Palace of Vivec.
