Makes the eastern part of Azura's Coast rockier, with Zafirbel Bay getting its own unique parasol mushroom variant.

Major landscape overhaul covering the eastern and parts of the southern Azura's Coast region.

## World Edits:

Adds new terrain and flora statics to the eastern Azura's Coast region, going south from Tel Mora, covering the Zarfirbel Bay, and down to Tel Branora. Does not include the areas directly north of Tel Mora, or the south western portion of the Azura's Coast.

Makes no interior cell edits and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with OAAB Shipwrecks, Azuras Shrine Overhaul, Daedric Travel Network, Ngasta Pasta, The Vanishing Ash-Chirps, Tall Dungeons, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, and virtually all town and city overhaul mods impacting Sadrith Mora, Tel Mora, Tel Aruhn, Tel Fyr, and Tel Branora.

A compatibility patch is available for Marbled Zarfirbel Bay.
