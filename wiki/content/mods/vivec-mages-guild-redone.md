> A rework and expansion of the Vivec Mages Guild making it feel more like the regional headquarters of a guild.

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of the Vivec, Mages Guild. Changes and expands the layout. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Comes with compatibility patches for Tamriel Rebuilt and The Guiding Guild. Otherwise, will conflict with mods like Vivec - God the City and Vivec - City of Swords, which make their own alterations to the Vivec Guild of Mages.
