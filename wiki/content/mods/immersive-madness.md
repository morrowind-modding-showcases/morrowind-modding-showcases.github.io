A quest mod. Makes Sheogorath cultists a joinable faction.

## World Edits:

Modifies the interiors of a number of Sheogorath-related Daedric shrines, primarily adding new quest objects and NPCs. Makes no significant changes to interiors and no landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

May have conflicts with some Daedric shrine overhauls, but overall, should be compatible with nearly all other mods.
