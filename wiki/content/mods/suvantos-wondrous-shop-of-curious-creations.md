This mod adds a new shop to the bottom-most level of the Dwemer ruin on the side of Red Mountain known as Bthanchend. The merchant, a Khajiit named Suvanto, sells some of the craziest weaponry and armor in Morrowind.<br /><br />Part of the 2016 Morrowind Modding Madness competition.
