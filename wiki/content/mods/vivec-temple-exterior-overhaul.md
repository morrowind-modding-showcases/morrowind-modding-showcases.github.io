> This is a standalone mod whose goal is to breathe a little more life into the exterior of the Vivec Temple Canton, which I'd always personally felt was rather bare on the outside in vanilla Morrowind. While other mods may aim to do this, the main point of this mod is to do so without redoing the entire city, allowing for further modularity. The download contains three versions, the differences of which are documented below:

## Features:

FULL Version (DO NOT USE WITH DRAMATIC VIVEC) includes:

- Banners added to the outside of the Temple Canton
- An obelisk monument in the empty corner of the canton facing the palace, with Vivecs name carved into it
- Benches on the sides of the Temple
- Flowerpots with Coda flowers around benches

BANNERS ONLY - VANILLA Version ( DO NOT USE THIS IF YOU USE DRAMATIC VIVEC) :

- Banners added to the outside of the Temple Canton

BANNERS ONLY - DRAMATIC VIVEC Version:

- Made specifically to guarantee compatibility between V.T.E.O and D.V.

## World Edits

Exterior clutter and detailing overhaul for the Vivec Temple canton. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.
