A small landscaping overhaul to the exterior of Salothan Ancestral Tomb drawing inspiration from the Salothan Council area of ESO.

## World Edits:

Adds terrain and architecture assets around the entrance of the Salothan Ancestral Tomb. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods and confirmed compatible with Rocky West Gash and Salothan Beacon. No known conflicts.
