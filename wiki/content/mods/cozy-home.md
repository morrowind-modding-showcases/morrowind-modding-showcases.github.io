This mod adds a small player home southeast of Seyda Neen just past the strider port and bandit cave. The home offers plenty of storage and space for you and your companion! No need to quest for or purchase this home; just enter and it's yours. 

## World Edits:

Modifies a single exterior cell with the addition of a new player home and related new interior.

Makes landscape edits to cell (-1, -9)

## Other Notes:

Compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees and OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat.

May otherwise have conflicts with mods that modify the landscape in cell (-1, -9).
