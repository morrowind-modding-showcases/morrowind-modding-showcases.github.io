> Family and House is a short story of family, legacy, tradition, and love. A slice of life for a very unordinary family. Told mostly through in-game notes and books, you will have to decide where your conscience lies.

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Ashlands region, along with minor additions to exterior and interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with Devilish Rocky Red Mountain, but a [compatibility patch is available here](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/60133?tab=description). No other known conflicts have been reported.
