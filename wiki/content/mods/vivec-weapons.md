> A weapon store in Vivec, Hlaalu Canton located on the exterior of the ground level that stocks a large selection of over thirty custom
> weapons. Swords knives, mace's, club's, axe's are all available for purchase.

## World Edits

Adds a new exterior entrance to a new interior cell to the Hlaalu Canton in Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other Vivec mods, minus those that move the Hlaalu Canton.
