> Clutters up Vivec's Palace so it now resembles the home of a god rather than the depressing chamber of emptiness we got in vanilla.

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of the Palace of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with all other mods, minus those directly touching upon the Palace of Vivec.
