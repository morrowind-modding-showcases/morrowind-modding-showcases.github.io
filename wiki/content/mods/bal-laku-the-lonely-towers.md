## Description:

This mod adds two towers to the northeastern Ashlands, using resources from The Hive - an unfinished beta from the Resdayn Revival Team. 

As of Version 2, this mod has been completely remade. It has been redesigned to better fit in thematically and stylistically with the mod: Tales from the Ashlands - The Great Hive Baan Binif by RandomPal and The Hive Mind team. While being redesigned around Baan Binif, Bal'laku still maintains its own distinct identity and can be played with or without Baan Binif. 

Aside from the changes to the towers themselves, there are two more major additions with Version 2. Bal'laku - The Lonely Towers now includes a landscape overhaul for a large chunk of the northern ashlands - roughly the area between Ebernanit in the west, and Bthuand in the east. The landscape overhaul has been achieved with zero edits to the height map to maintain maximum compatibility with other mods. 

The other major addition is a new NPC at the towers who has a lot of unique dialogue as well a short questline that partially ties into the main quest. This questline also expands a bit upon the lore surrounding a minor character from vanilla Morrowind.

There is also a new book about the towers (using the lore write-up from the mod description) that can be found in a couple places around Vvardenfell.

## World Edits:

Primarily a landscape overhaul adding new terrain and detailing assets to the eastern Northern Ashlands. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.
