Another take on Demanufacturer87s Markgran Forest mod. Covers the area near Maar Gan in a new mushroom forest.

## World Edits:

Landscape overhaul of the Ashlands around Maar Gan, adding new flora and terrain assets. Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Based on Markgran Forest and will consequently conflict with that mod, will otherwise be compatible with most Maar Gan settlement overhauls.

A compatibility patch for [Red Mountain Reborn is available here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/54460)
