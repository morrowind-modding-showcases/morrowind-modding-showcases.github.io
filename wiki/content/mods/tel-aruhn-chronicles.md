Large scale overhaul of Tel Aruhn and Yansirramus Shrine with complete OAAB integration, new quests, and a lot more. A mod submitted for the 2020 Morrowind Modding Madness competition.

## Features:
- Tel Aruhn overhaul with full OAAB_data integration for both interiors and exteriors
- Massive expansion of Tel Aruhn's Underground
- Unique dialogue for various NPCs in Tel Aruhn
- New quests
- Yansirramus shrine overhaul
- A new set of Telvanni Slaver armor by Ashtaar

## World Edits:

Interior and exterior overhaul of the settlement of Tel Aruhn, with new detailing, clutter, and a couple of new buildings added to the exterior cells of Tel Aruhn, while the interiors have likewise been updated with new assets.

Includes landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

This mod has been merged into Zafirbel Beacons. Will conflict with Verdant Tel Aruhn, but otherwise should be compatible with most other mods.
