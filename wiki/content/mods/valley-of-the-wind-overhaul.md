Overhauls the Valley of the Wind with massive wind torn cliffs and fallen trees.

## World Edits:

Landscape overhaul covering the north-eastern part of the Ashlands, along the border with the Grazelands, including new terrain assets and land edits.

## Other Notes:

Should be largely compatible with other mods, few other landscape overhauls impact this particular location. Confirmed compatible with OAAB Grazelands.
