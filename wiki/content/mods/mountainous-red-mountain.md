Increases the height of Red Mountain to almost 1.6 times original height and gives the landscape a more spikey volcanic fantasy look.

## World Edits:

Extensive changes to the exterior cells of Red Mountain, including widespread landscape edits in virtually all Red Mountain cells and surrounding areas.

## Other Notes:

Beyond world edits, also replaces the vanilla game meshes for Red Mountain rocks, which are used across the game-world. Consequently, will conflict with a large number of mods without a compatibility patch.
