Telvanni tower grew over Mawia. Drive out the undead and keep it for yourself, such is the way of Telvanni.

## World Edits:

Adds new details and statics to the exterior of the Velothi tower of Mawia, as well as a light overhaul of the interior and new interiors.

Also impacts a number of non-Mawia vanilla interiors, but these are dirty edits and can safely be removed by utilities like Enchanted Editor or TES3CMD without impacting the primary focus of the mod.

## Other Notes:

Dirty edits aside, should be compatible with most other mods, including most other mods in the Azura's Coast.
