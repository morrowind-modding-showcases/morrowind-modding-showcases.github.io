> Overhauls the Vivec Temple exterior in the style of Dramatic Vivec, but with fewer conflicts, fewer bugs, and more frames.

## World Edits

Exterior only overhaul of the Vivec Temple canton, with new assets and clutter. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with other Vivec temple canton overhauls and Dramatic Vivec, but confirmed compatible with Passage of Prayers, and most other Vivec addition and clutter mods.
