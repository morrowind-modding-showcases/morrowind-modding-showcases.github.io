> This mod adds new pirate themed quests, items and locations to Morrowind. Including an Imperial style tradehouse and a shady smugglers' den where you can embark on several quests and explore new underwater dungeons throughout the seas.

## World Edits

Adds a number of new locations, including a new settlement with exterior clutter and detailing to the edge of the Molag Amur region, and a handful of dungeons out to sea. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, no major conflicts reported.
