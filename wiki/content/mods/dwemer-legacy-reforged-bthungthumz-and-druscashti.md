Cinematic overhaul of obsolete dwemer ruins: Bthungthumz and Druscashti. Will you dare to enter this kingdom of ruin and search for ancient treasures? Will you dare to interrupt eternal torment of restless soul?... Legacy of the Dwemer awaits!

## World Edits:

Primarily a landscape overhaul outside the Dwemer ruins of Bthungthumz and Druscashti. Adds new clutter, statics, and makes landscape edits to the areas around each ruin.

Also includes one new interior.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with all other known mods in the northern Ashlands. No known conflicts at this time.
