> This mod adds a whole new guild to Morrowind, which will have you questing for knowledge and rare artifacts, long lost.
> You eventually gain the knowledge on how to become a Lich.

## World Edits

Adds a number of new locations across Vvardenfell with associated exterior entrances, clutter, and landscape edits. Also adds new quest-related objects and NPCs to a variety of vanilla locations.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with a number of other mods, though an [improved compatibility version](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/54814) is available with support for BCOM, Balmora Outskirts, Rocky West Gash, Secrets of the Crystal City, OAAB - Tombs and Towers, and Ruined Molag Mar.
