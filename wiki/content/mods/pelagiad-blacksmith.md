> An Imperial Smith has set up shop in Fort Pelagiad and is seeking supplies from Ebonheart.

## World Edits

Adds clutter and detailing to the Fort Pelagiad courtyard, along with some minor additions to Ebonheart. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to interior cells.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with a number of Pelagiad city overhauls, particularly those that change or detail the courtyard. Should be compatible with most other mods.
