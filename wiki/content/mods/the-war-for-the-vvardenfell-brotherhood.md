Indulge in a tale of the Dark Brotherhood's eastern civil war, the rise of the Mythic Dawn, and the fall of Severa Magia.

## Introduction

This mod allows you to join the Vvardenfell branch of the Dark Brotherhood in Morrowind.

You'll find yourself recruited into a faction caught in a war with itself and the Morag Tong, while also trying to appease allies in the growing Cult of Mehrunes Dagon. Nothing is ever as it seems in this story. There are many hidden truths and roles you can play in the chaos that is the War for the Vvardenfell Brotherhood. But be careful - the Prophet of Sithis is hunting you from the shadows. The Shadowscale Usha-Madesi has you in his sights, and he will stop at nothing to see the destruction of you and the brotherhood that has strayed too far from the tenants.

This is not just a Dark Brotherhood story, this is very much a story leading up to the events of The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion. This mod also references works from other Crown projects.

Within the story, there are four possible angles you can take:

You can be a loyal servant of Sithis, brought into the fray of uncertain times.
Or, maybe an assassin-turned follower of Mehrunes Dagon, loyal to the Camorans.
Or, more simply, you could be a killer, simply out for their own benefit of power.
Or, maybe secretly, an agent of Mephala, determined with taking actions into their own hands.

There are four different possible endings, all of which preserve canon, and therefore by my own definition - this mod is lore-friendly.

## Features

30 new quests.
Multiple endings.
A new joinable faction.
New interesting characters.
New lore that fits seamlessly with vanilla.
Optional decorated shrines for Mehrunes Dagon.

## World Edits:

Adds quest objects and NPCs to a variety of interiors and locations. Includes an optional add-on overhauling the shrines to Mehrunes Dagon.

## Other Notes:

Has compatibility patches for Maar Gan - Town of Pilgrimage, Morag Tong Polished, and Tamriel Rebuilt.

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind and Daedric Shrine Overhaul FULL (assuming one isn't using the optional overhaul addon).
