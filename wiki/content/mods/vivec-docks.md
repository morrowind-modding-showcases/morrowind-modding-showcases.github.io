> This mod adds some new docks with a couple of buildings and a few NPCs to Vivec. These docks are attached to the Foreign Quarter Canton. I've tried to keep everything believable, balanced, and performance friendly. These docks can be accessed by a door in the Canalworks.

## World Edits

Expands the Vivec Foreign Quarter Canton with new structures and clutter, including landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with a number of Vivec city overhauls, specifically those that move the Foreign Quarter canton. Might be compatible with those that keep the Foreign Quarter canton in the vanilla location.
