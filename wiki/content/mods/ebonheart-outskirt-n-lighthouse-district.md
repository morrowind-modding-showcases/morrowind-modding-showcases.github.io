> A new outskirt in Ebonheart. Plus a Lighthouse small fisher district. And a player home

## World Edits

Adds new buildings, clutter, and associated interiors to the peninsula south of Ebonheart. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with mods that expand Ebonheart or add to the area south of Ebonheart. Probably compatible with Witness Structure, but untested.

A compatibility version for Vivec Lighthouse c[an be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/57903)
