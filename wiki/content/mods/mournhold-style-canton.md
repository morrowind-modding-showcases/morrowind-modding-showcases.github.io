> A new artificial island or canton added to Vivec City in the style of Mournhold.

## Features:

- New Canton model in the style of Mournhold.
- 4 buildings situated in the outdoor plaza, including a Magic Shop and Tavern.
- A large player house with a loads storage.
- Does not alter the landscape to increase compatibility with other mods.
- Over 50 new models including structures, furniture and clutter, made in Blender.
- Cleaned with TESAME.

  New Features added in 2.0:
- A second smaller player home.
- Both properties can now be bought through bartering, taking account of the player's Mercantile skill.
- Rooms have been added to the tavern for rent.
- A gondolier to ferry the player around Vivec, with his own workshop.
- The Canton canalworks in the Mournhold style.
- All NPC have Background and My Trade Dialogues.
- A new NPC home.

## World Edits

Adds a new canton to an exterior cell on the edge of the Norvayn Bay with associated new structures, clutter, and new interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed compatible with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage and confirmed conflicts with Witness Structure - The Great Seawall of Vivec and New Arenthia. Compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
