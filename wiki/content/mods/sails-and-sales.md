Become the captain of your own ship, recruit crew members and acquire cargo to sell.<br />At long last, you may sail the Seas or row on the lakes and rivers!

## Features of this mod include:

- Your own ship that you can sail in real-time and/or fast travel. Visit the various ports of Vvardenfell or just sail around at sea.
- The opportunity to acquire smaller vessels, such as rowboats and skiffs to increase the size of your fleet.
- A surprising introductory quest.
- 20+ quests for you to do. Be ready to travel, the quest will take you all over Vvardenfell.
- Complete tasks for the various merchants of Vvardenfell to acquire cargo for your ship that you can sell and make a profit.
- Recruit several colourful and interesting characters for your crew and keep them content completing their quests.
- Some of your crew can become companions.
- Twin Lamps quests that let you decide where your loyalty lies.
- Special attention to your character's past: quests involving the (vanilla) npc you talk to, your rank in guilds, your skills, your choices, and progress with some vanilla quests will change how our quests are played and how the npcs react to you.

## World Edits:

Primarily a quest mod, makes edits to a number of different locations and interior cells, adding quest objects and NPCs.

Also overhauls and/or expands the vanilla game dungeons of Abesen-Pulu Egg Mine, Sinsibadon, and Ainat.

## Other Notes:

Comes with a compatibility patch for Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Will be compatible with most mods that don't overhaul or massively change the same interiors.
