## Description:

Morrowind is full of ships, boats and skiffs but who built them and where? Our mod seeks to answer these questions once and for all by adding shipyards to towns by the sea. The mod adds shipyards to Sadrith Mora, Seyda Neen and Gnaar Mok with four quests in total. 

Shipyards are vital for factions to ensure smooth travel and transportation across Vvardenfell and beyond. However, they are also ideal targets of sabotage to undermine a faction's influence on a region.

## World Edits:

Adds new buildings and clutter to the edges of the towns of Seyda Neen, Sadrith Mora, and Gnaar Mok, as well as a handful of other quest-related locations to exterior cells.

Includes other quest-related edits to vanilla locations, as well as a landscape edit in a single cell.

Will conflict with any mod that heavily modifies the area directly north of Seyda Neen, such as Little Landscapes - Seyda Neen Wetlands and B.B.C. Better Bitter Coast.
