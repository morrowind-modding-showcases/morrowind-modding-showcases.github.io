An extensive companion and quest mod that hooks onto Morrowinds Main Quest and offers a serious perspective to the world presented. Team up with a complex character with her own faults, hopes, and dreams.

## World Edits:

Modifies and makes edits to a number of vanilla locations, primarily adding quest objects and/or NPCs, but also overhauling some vanilla game dungeons with some other interior expansions.

Includes landscape edits in the eastern Ashlands with a new Buoyant Armiger stronghold. Also adds a couple of other new locations, one in the Ascadian Isles and one in the Molag Amur regions.

## Compatibility:

A large number of compatibility patches exist for Arvesa, and it should be compatible with most mods as a result. Will conflict with other mods making overhauls to the same dungeons though.
