Adds a Telvanni lighthouse to the waters near Sadrith Mora and Tel Aruhn.

## World Edits:

Adds a single new location to the Azura's Coast region in the Zafirbel Bay, northeast of Tel Aruhn, with some new exterior clutter and detailing.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with virtually all other mods. Has since been merged into and expanded by [Tel Ahini](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59003).
