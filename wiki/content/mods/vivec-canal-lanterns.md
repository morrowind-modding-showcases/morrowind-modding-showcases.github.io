> This mod adds some floating paper lanterns into the canals of Vivec City, including the ones in Vivec's Temple. There are less lanterns as you get farther from the temple.

## World Edits

Adds new light sources to the canals of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other Vivec city mods that don't move the Vivec cantons. Will likely conflict with Vivec Expansion.
