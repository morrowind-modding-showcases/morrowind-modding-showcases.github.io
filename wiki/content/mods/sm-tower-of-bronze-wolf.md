> The mysterious tower of the Bronze Wolf, in the west of the village of Gnisis, near the Koala Cave.
> After visiting the tower and dealing with its owners, the player will be able to use it as his home or transshipment point
> However, this is not a classic mod in the style of "home for the player", there are no convenient chests, a dozen cabinets and dummies for armor. It's just an old gloomy Western-style tower.
> However, there are still some interesting things in it.

## Features:

- There is a small cave under the tower where you can hide all your secrets or get out unnoticed. The cave has three entrances/exits.
- Also, from the cave (by clicking on the boat) you can get to a small pier in the vicinity of the village.
- The light in the windows varies depending on the time of day. Thus, being in the tower and going to the window, you will always know the day or night in the outside.
- The "heavenly portal" behaves in a similar way which is located above the roof. It is visible only at night. Probably, the owner of the tower draws some energy from Nirn, for his "magical affairs".
- As for the rest of the portals: inside the tower there is a system of portals "down and up", the owner does not like to walk too much, especially overcoming thousands of steps. However, if you have a desire to run up the stairs, you can do it (ignoring portals) All stairs in the tower are fully operational and passable.
- On the top floor, by activating a special magic item, you can use the "pocket dimension" in which the owner of the tower kept all sorts of junk. The container in the form of a portal is quite roomy.
- The owner of this tower belongs to a very specific race. Better check it yourself.
- Oh yes, having dealt with all the enemies, do not forget to solve the "skeletons problem" on the lower floor. Just take the green skull under the spiral at the bottom...

## World Edits

Adds a new location off the coast of the West Gash, near Koal Cave. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods, and confirmed compatible with Rocky West Gash.
