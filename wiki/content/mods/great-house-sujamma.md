> Breaking out of his shell, the talking mudcrab merchant has vowed to start a Great House of his very own! Speak to him, and you may become a part of his grand endeavor.

## Features:

- A complete faction questline to play through!
- 26 quests (20 Main Quests and 6 Side Quests), all based around the affairs of Great House Sujamma.
- Tons of new assets, including a unique model for the Mudcrab Merchant himself!
- New weapons, armor, and miscellaneous items to play around with, including a final reward sword that has a sheath! (For those ﻿with the sheath mod).
- A community of rejects who flock to House Sujamma because they have no where else to go.
- A big ol' guildhall that changes as you advance through the questline. This serves equally as a player home and staging base ﻿for ﻿your operations.

## World Edits

Adds a new building, constructed during stages, with associated exterior clutter, to the Mudcrab Merchant's island in the Azura's Coast. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with mods modifying the same island, but otherwise should be compatible with all other mods.
