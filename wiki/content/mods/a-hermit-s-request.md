> To the west of Seyda Neen, along the Bitter Coast, there
> lives hermit by the name of Thexn Akaasul. It would seem that Thexn has a
> series of tasks for any adventurous soul willing to seek him out, though they
> deal mostly with the necessities of life, rather than adventures of the
> grandiose sort.
>
> But perhaps there is more here than meets the eye? There's
> something not quite right about this hermit, something hidden and kept in the
> dark. Will you be the one to shed light on the matter? Will you help Thexn
> Akaasul uncover the thing that he desires most? And when you find that which
> Thexn seeks, will you bestow it upon him or take it away? Well now, that's up
> to you, isn't it?

## Features:

- Six new quests surrounding the hermit of Thexn Akaasul in the Bitter Coast.
- One new dungeon, gradually unlocked during different stages of Thexn's questline
- Quest rewards designed for early level characters, though potentially higher level characters might find some of them useful.
- Some dialogue integration with the residents of Seyda Neen.
- Some quests have multiple potential solutions, depending on player skill.
- Over 11k words of new dialogue, documents, and journal entries, with new dialogue responses for Thexn the better you get to know him.
- One new potential player home.
- Compatibility Versions for Morrowind Rebirth and Ashfront - Bitter Coast.

## World Edits

Adds a new building and a new dungeon entrance to the Bitter Coast, including new associated interior cells. Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors and NO landscape edits.
