Reworks the StoneWood pass

## World Edits:

Adds terrain assets, clutter and detailing, including landscape edits, to the exterior cells between Balmora and Caldera.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

A number of compatibility patches exist for Foyada Mamaea, Traveling Merchants, Riders, and Caldera Mine Simplified. 

Confirmed compatible with Balmora - Seat of Power and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Additional patches are available for [Izi Cities - Caldera](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50564) and [OAAB Saplings](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/50626).

Should also be compatible with various alternative versions of Rocky West Gash, such as Devilish Rocky West Gash.

Will conflict with Odai - Lifeblood of Balmora and the more complete version of Caldera Mine Expanded.
