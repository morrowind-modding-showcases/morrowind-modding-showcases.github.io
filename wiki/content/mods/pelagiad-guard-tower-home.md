> This mod allows the player to obtain a home located in Pelagiad.

## Features:

- Alchemy Sorter & De-sorter
- Spell Scroll Sorter
- Potion Sorter
- Teleportation amulet
- Lock-pick training desk
- Plenty of storage

## World Edits

Minor exterior edits to Pelagiad, primarily adding some detailing to the Pelagiad Guard tower, along with a new interior. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with mods that move the Pelagiad Guard Tower. Should be compatible with all other mods.
