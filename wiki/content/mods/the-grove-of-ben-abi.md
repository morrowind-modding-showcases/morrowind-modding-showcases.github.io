This small mod is an overhaul to the memorable mushroom grove near Dagon Fel. It adds a lot of detail as well as a background story to this area.

## World Edits:

Adds flora and clutter to the central valley of Sheogorad's largest island, including landscape edits.

Makes NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with most Sheogorad mods, but notably has since been used as either inspiration or merged wholesale into other landscape overhauls, and will conflict with those mods.
