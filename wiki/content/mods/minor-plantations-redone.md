> Ever thought that the Gro-Bagrat and Arano plantations felt more like farmhouses instead of proper plantations?
> This mod aims to fix that by expanding them. New buildings, revised interiors, a few new NPCs, and more!

## World Edits

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of the Gro-Bagrat and Arano Plantations, including new structures with associated new interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

A compatibility patch is available for Ascadia Farmyard Overhaul (through that mod's download page). Otherwise, should be compatible with Concept Arts Plantations.
