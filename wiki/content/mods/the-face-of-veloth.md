This mod adds the Face of Veloth as a discoverable location into the game. It also overhauls the landscape of an island in the Azura's Coast Region, where the face can be found.

## World Edits:

Overhauls an island in the Azura's Coast region with new terrain assets and detailing. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Will have conflicts with other mods using the same location. Notably a few house mods do use that particular island in the Azura's Coast. Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods.
