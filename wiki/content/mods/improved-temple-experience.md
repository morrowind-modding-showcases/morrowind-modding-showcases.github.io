> Adds shrines to the temples in Vos, Suran, Maar Gan and Molag Mar.
> Adds teleport markers for Almvisi Intervention to Vos, Suran, Maar Gan and Ghostgate.

## World Edits

Adds shrines to various temple interiors and places invisible intervention markers to various temple exteriors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods and includes alternative versions for compatibility with RR - Ghostgate Fortress and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
