> Moves the Telvanni Canton of Vivec slightly eastward and removes the lower level bridges to said canton to make Vivec City more traversable by large boats and ships.

## World Edits

Edits and move the exterior of the Telvanni canton in Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed conflict with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, and likely conflicts with most Vivec city overhauls that touch the exterior of the Telvanni canton.
