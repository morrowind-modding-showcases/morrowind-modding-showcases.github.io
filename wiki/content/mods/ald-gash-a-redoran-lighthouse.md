This mod adds a Redoran lighthouse that also served as a small base of operation for their troops patrolling the Inner Sea. Now it only has a skeleton crew, since most of the men were relocated to fight the threat of the Sixth House. Still, the tower stands, lighting the way for ships travelling those dangerous waters. If you pay the residents a visit, you may be able to help some with matters that trouble them...

## World Edits:

Adds a new building, statics, and landscape edits to the West Gash region near the Daedric shrine of Yasammidan.

Makes no edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

A compatibility patch is [available for Rocky West Gash here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/56060)

Otherwise, highly compatible with most other mods.
