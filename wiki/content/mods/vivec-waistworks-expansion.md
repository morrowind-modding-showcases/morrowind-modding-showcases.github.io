Expands the Vivec Waistworks with new shops, shrines, restaurants, and decorations.

## World Edits:

Overhauls and expands the waistworks of each of the major cantons of Vivec with new supplemental interiors, detailing, and clutter.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with any other mods altering the Vivec Waistworks.

This mod was merged into [Compatible Redesigned Vivec.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49336)
