> Hours of gameplay, a dangerous RESCUE MISSION, a strange ARTIFACT, ever-lasting COMPANIONSHIP, odd CHARACTERS and ROMANCE for male as well as female PC Characters - you'll find it all in the Witchgirl Adventure.

## World Edits

Adds a number of new locations, including new buildings and dungeons, as well as quest-related additions to some vanilla interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with other mods based in Ald'ruhn and Hla Oad.
