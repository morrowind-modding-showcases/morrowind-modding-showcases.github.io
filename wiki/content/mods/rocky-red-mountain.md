This mod makes Red Mountain more rocky. The mod does not affect the Red Mountain region, but the geographical object of the same name. Oh yes. Now you need to be careful near active volcano vents and lava lakes.

## World Edits:

Massive landscape overhaul for Red Mountain, covering every exterior cell of the Red Mountain region and many surrounding cells from the Ashlands. Includes new terrain assets and landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

This mod is used as the basis for two major variations of Rocky Red Mountain designed to be more compatible with other mods - for otherwise it'll have a lot of conflicts.

These include:
- [Devilish Rocky Red Mountain](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/53473)
- [Rocky Red Mountain Decidedly Compatible Version](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/53398?tab=files&file_id=1000039835)
