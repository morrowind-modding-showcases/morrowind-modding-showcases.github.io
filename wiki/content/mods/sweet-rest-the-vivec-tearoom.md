> A small tavern type locale in Vivec, with custom NPCs and a small quest. Mod also includes a tomb with custom item reward.

## World Edits

Adds a new interior connected to the Vivec Foreign Quarter Waistworks, as well as a new tomb in the Ascadian Isles with associated landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have landscape conflicts with other mods affecting the same exterior cells. Probably compatible with most other mods.
