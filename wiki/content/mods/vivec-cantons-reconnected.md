> Adds ladder and trapdoors connecting the waistworks and plazas of each canton so the player does not have to exit and re-enter the canton.

## World Edits

Adds new trapdoors between interior cells of the Vivec cantons. Makes NO exterior cell edits and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Compatible with most other mods that don't open the canton plazas or change the layout of the canton waistworks.
