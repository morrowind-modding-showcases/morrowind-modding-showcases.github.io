> Castle Ebonheart and the Imperial Chapel interiors have been refurbished, now featuring OAAB and TR assets. The castle should now resemble a proper ducal estate and seat of imperial power in Vvardenfell.

## Features:

- A new tower for the chapel, complete with stained glass and a ceremonial font.
- The Red Dome Templar has a minor presense in the Imperial Chapels, where they hoard and protect their secrets within the chapel crypts.
- There is now a place where the different members of the Duke's council can sit down and argue, as well as improved decor, courtesy of T_D and OAAB.
- The Imperial Commission's offices and shrine have been improved, giving it a more practical appearance.
- The Duke now has a place to sleep.
- New world interactions, including the option to return the stolen Legion artifacts to Cavortius of the Imperial Guard garrison so that they can be restored in the shrine, or to see the chapel limeware bowl back in its place once it's been returned.
- Some NPCs have had their inventories or characters updated, with more fitting outfits, equipment or even faces given to them.

## World Edits

Makes interior and exterior edits to Castle Ebonheart, primarily overhauling the interiors of the castle, but also adding new detailing to the exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with a number of Ebonheart city overhauls. Confirmed compatible with Ebonheart Underworks.
