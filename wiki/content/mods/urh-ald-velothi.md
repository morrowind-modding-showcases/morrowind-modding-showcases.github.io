The Ald Velothi Outpost is a sanctuary for travelers and explorers in the West Gash Region. This mod changes the appearance of the hut and tower with a new model. Together with some detailed cluttering, featuring new made meshes and OAAB meshes, it turns the outpost into a unique Redoran experience.

## Features
- A new hut and tower mesh, both exterior as well as interior.
- Detailed cluttering with dedicated meshes and meshes from OAAB data.
- Added two new rooms for the absent Redoran nobleman and his daughter to stay.

## World Edits:

Replaces the Ald Velothi outpost, both interior and exterior, including landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Includes patches for Animated Morrowind and Patch for Purists. Has a minor conflict with Grim Khuul and Enchanted Ald Velothi. Confirmed compatible with Velothi Ald Velothi, Properly Cluttered Ald Velothi, Vvardenfell Brotherhood, and Holidays Across Tamriel.

Note: Has been merged into Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
