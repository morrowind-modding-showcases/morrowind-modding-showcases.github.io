A new shop has opened in Sadrith Mora. People from all over Vvardenfell travel here to purchase the famous Telvanni bug musk. BCOM compatible. Not only will you get an insight into the secrets of bug musk alchemy but you'll also help the perfumist, Dalmil Seloth, find the culprit who desecrated and plundered the tomb of his family.

## Features:
- A Telvanni bug musk shop
- A desecrated ancestral tomb
- Two additional merchants at the Sadrith Mora marketplace
- Unique clothing assets
- One quest to combine them all

## World Edits:

Adds new exterior buildings and dungeon entrances to and near Sadrith Mora, with associated exterior clutter.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Morrowind Rebirth, Sadrith Mora - Seat of Power, and OAAB Market.

Note: This mod has been merged into Sadrith Mora BCOM Plus Module.
