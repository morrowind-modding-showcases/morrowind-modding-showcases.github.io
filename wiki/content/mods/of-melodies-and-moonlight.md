Surrounded by rumors, a peculiar painter roams the night. Will you help him and become his apprentice? To start the quest, talk to Veradul Dervayn, the artist, who for some odd reason prefers to paint at night. BCOM compatible. The painter will appear after 24 hours the first time you visit him. MWSE and OpenMW compatible.

## Features:

-A peculiar artist with a long quest
﻿-A quest for the artist with two detailed quest branches leading to two different endings.
﻿-Adds two unique housepods to Sadrith Mora with detailed interiors. 
﻿-High quality Morrowind art by Igor Levchenko!
﻿-You can create paintings for nobles if you side with the painter! 

## World Edits:

Quest mod featuring new buildings with associated interiors in Sadrith Mora, including some exterior clutter.

Also makes edits to a number of other interiors and exteriors, largely with quest objects and/or NPCs.

Includes lightweight overhaul of the final room of the dungeon of Kora-Dur.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, OAAB Market and Sadrith Mora - Seat of Power.

Note: This mod has been merged into Sadrith Mora BCOM Plus Module.

Confirmed conflict with Coldharbour - Palace of Menta Na
