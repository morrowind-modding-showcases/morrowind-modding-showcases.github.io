Significant overhaul of the exterior and interior of Dagoth Ur, transforming it into a fortress befitting its legendary status.<br />Ancient technology and the power of the heart of Lorkhan will present new challenges.

## World Edits:

Edits and overhauls the exteriors and landscapes of Red Mountain and the Dwemer citadels on it, as well as makes edits to a number of vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly incompatible with other Red Mountain mods.

Notably never actually completed, the mod is currently in an unfinished state.
