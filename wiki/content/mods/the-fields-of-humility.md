A minor, atmospheric landscape overhaul for the Fields of Kummu.

## Overview:

This mod overhauls the Fields of Kummu into a unique micro-biome where mucksponge grows in abundance. It adds a few other points of interest as well:

- A slightly more detailed shrine
- A new prayer wall near the shrine 
- A pilgrim camp just to the north of the shrine
- Scenic lantern strings lighting the paths near the camp and shrine
- Exterior additions to the nearby Sarano Ancestral Tomb
- The ancient ruins of the poor farmer's farmhouse

This mod also adds a new, short quest. To begin, speak to the pilgrim near the shrine.

## World Edits:

Exterior only overhaul of the Fields of Kummu, adding new clutter, statics, and flora to the exterior cells in the northern Ascadian Isles.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Conflicts with Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage, but otherwise has compatibility with Seven Graces Shrines Enhanced, and should be compatible with most other mods in the Ascadian Isles region.
