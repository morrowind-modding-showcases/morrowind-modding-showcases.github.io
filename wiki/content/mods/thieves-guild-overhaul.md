> A complete overhaul of the Thieves' Guild faction. New quests, tweaks for existing quests, items, and a lot more!

## World Edits

Adds quest NPCs and objects to a number of exterior and interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods. May have minor conflicts with mods that change building locations or change the layout of the Corprusarium.
