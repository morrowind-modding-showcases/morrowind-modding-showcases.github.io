## Overview

Overhauls and lightly expands on the major tribal camps along with lightly adding more clutter to the exterior of the minor Ashlander camps that are found dotted around Vvardenfell, This adds a good amount of stuff, I've tried to keep it as vanilla as possible with lore on the tribes and their major camp. 

Also worth noting I've not edited any of the landscapes  so any ground covers will be safe, along with editing the pathfinding do npcs and companions dont get stuck on things I've added.


Ahemmusa Camp - Herding & Hunting Camp
- Adds a small area for hunter's to practice their bow skills, Guar & Shalk Herding pen area, More tanning racks, A skiff near the coastal water side of the camp for fishing, A small in progress damaged wall around the camp built by the tribe too combat the animal attacks on the camp.

Erabenimsun Camp - Hostile & Warlike
- Added some ash log barricades to resemble the war-side of the tribe using broken trees found around the area along with more flora to show the struggles of the bad environment the camp deals with.

Urshilaku Camp - Cultural & Keeps To Traditions
- Adds more of a comfort and secure like feeling too the camp, Adds afew guar like bones to the camp as they guide the tribe, even after death.

Zainab Camp - Lands For Trade & Harvest
- Adds a pack guar with afew carts and "Cargo" also some stored general wares like crates and barrels along with an area for refining royal wax harvested by kwama queens for the tribes various crafting and religious ceremonies.
