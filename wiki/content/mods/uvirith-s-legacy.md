> This mod turns Tel Uvirith into a fitting home for a lord and a stunning symbol of the Archmagister's power.
>
> This third generation Tel Uvirith mod, based on Uvirith Inside, does what Uvirith Awakened was trying to do before the project fell into limbo.
>
> New quests, new areas to explore, new magical artifacts, new tower services, and updated Uvirith Inside content, are all designed to make the daily life of a Telvanni lord more interesting.

## World Edits

Overhauls the interiors of Tel Uvirith, in addition to various interior and exterior cell edits with quest NPCs, objects, and new locations. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with other Tel Uvirith interior overhauls, but otherwise, a number of compatibility patches exist for most popular mods, and confirmed to be compatible with Building Up Uvirith's Legacy, Rise of House Telvanni, and LGNPC Tel Uvirith.

Note: This wiki page is based on Uvirith's Legacy 3.53. A newer 4.0 version was in development in 2019, but never released. Please update this page should a new version be posted.
