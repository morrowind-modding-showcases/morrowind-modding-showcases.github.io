A traveling bard spins a story of love and loss by magically casting you as the lead role. Act out the tragic tale as words come to life. Who knows? Maybe you'll even get to write the ending.

## Features:

A Familiar Song is a narrative quest mod guided by the tales of a mysterious traveling bard. Look out for a pensive Dunmer on the Dwemer bridge near Fort Moonmoth to get started.

This quest takes a unique approach to gameplay, as you'll quickly discover. I don't recommend using a fresh level 1 character - it's probably better to start once you hit the level 5-10 range.

Depending on how you approach the ending, you might end up with a very unique scripted magic item and a once-daily power, in addition to a few other odds and ends.

## World Edits:

Mostly adds minor additions to a variety of exterior cells in the form of quest objects and/or NPCs. Plus one entrance to a new dungeon.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Should be compatible with almost virtually everything. No known conflicts reported, though apparently Morrowind Achievements Collection can cause issues.
