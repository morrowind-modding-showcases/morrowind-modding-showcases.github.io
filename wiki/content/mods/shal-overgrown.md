Travel to Shal and discover its secrets: the once small cave is now overgrown and even reaches into the nearby Daedric ruin Ashurnibibi.

## World Edits:

Complete overhaul of the vanilla game dungeon of Shal. Also edits the nearby ruin of Ashurnibibi with a new interior dungeon expansion.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, will likely only conflict with other cavern overhauls of Shal.
