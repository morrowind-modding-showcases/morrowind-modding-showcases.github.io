> Vanilla-style stairs from Waistworks to Plazas for cantons of Vivec.

## World Edits

Makes minor edits to the layouts of the interior cells for the Vivec canton waistworks and plazas. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with other mods that change the layout of the canton waistworks or open the canton plazas. Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods.
