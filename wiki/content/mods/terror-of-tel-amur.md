> Adds a series of heists and a quest to investigate a mysterious new tower deep in the Molag Amur wilderness

## Features:

- A series of heists and hijinks overseen by Erer Darothril
- A mysterious new tower deep in Molag Amur
- New artifacts to discover
- New enemies to fight
- A closer look at a magical mechanic that's glossed over in the vanilla game
- A potential new player home
- Custom sounds and music by tewlwolow

## World Edits

Adds a new location atop a mountain in the Molag Amur region, including exterior clutter and detailing. Also features some quest-related NPCs and objects in vanilla interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Confirmed conflict with Molag Amur Mountains, unless using the compatibility patch included in the Molag Amur Mountains download. Otherwise, compatible with most other mods in the Molag Amur region, including Great Scathes and The Mountain of Fear.
