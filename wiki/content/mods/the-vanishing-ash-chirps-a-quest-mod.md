Deep in the Grazelands, a Khajiit scholar has gone missing while studying the mysterious Ashlander culture. Rumors suggest she sought out a reclusive hermit for answers. Can you unravel the secrets she uncovered?

## Features:
- a few fetch quests with a twist
- perfect for mid-level characters
- a chance to uncover the past of the Grazelands region in a unique way
- help a solitary Ashlander regain a lost companion
- spiders and bugs (if it's a big nope for you, consider using Arachnophobia Patch﻿)
- a custom icon for Skyrim Style Quest Notifications

## World Edits:

Adds some new locations to the exterior cells of the southern Grazelands, along with some new dungeon interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods and confirmed compatible with The Song of the Grazelands, OAAB Grazelands, The Ashlanders, and Azura's Coast Redone.
