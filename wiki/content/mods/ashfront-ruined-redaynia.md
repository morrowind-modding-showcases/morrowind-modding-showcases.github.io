Implements the cut village of Ald Redaynia and overhauls the nearby Velothi tower it's named for, expanding on existing content as well as adding an optional unmarked quest.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interior of the tower of Ald Redaynia, adds a new village and statics to the exterior.

Also makes landscape edits to the surrounding area of Ald Redaynia.

## Other Notes:

Requires Ashfront Sheogorad.

Will conflict with any other Ald Redaynia mod.

[Compatibility Patch available for Death & Taxes.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59601)
