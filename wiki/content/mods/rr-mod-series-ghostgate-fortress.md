New models and textures for Ghostgate and Ghostfence pylons based on Morrowind concept art.

## World Edits:

Overhauls and expands the fortress settlement of Ghostgate at the base of Red Mountain, including new buildings, model replacers, and landscape editing. Also overhauls the interiors of Ghostgate.

## Other Notes:

Includes a compatibility patch for Julan - Ashlander Companion. A number of other compatibility patches exist, including for OAAB Foyada Mamaea, Devilish Red Mountain, Red Mountain Reborn, and many other Ashlands and Red Mountain mods.

Will likely conflict with mods impacting the same area without a compatibility patch.
