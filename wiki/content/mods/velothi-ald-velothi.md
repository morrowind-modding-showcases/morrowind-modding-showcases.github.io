> *"When Ald Velothi was built, none can say. Its docks have clung to the West Gash coast since the Interregnum — perhaps longer."
> *
> Before the Empire came, before the Houses drew their lines across Vvardenfell, there was Ald Velothi. Old Velothi, in the truest sense — a settlement raised by the followers of the Prophet himself, its stones older than the memory of those who walk them.
>
> Time has not been kind. The trade routes moved on, the Temple's gaze drifted elsewhere, and now only fishermen and a small Redoran presence remain among the crumbling towers of their ancestors.
>
> Features:
>
> Remakes the settlement of Ald Velothi in the Velothi tileset. Ienas Arvel, whose shack was always in the game is now an NPC that offers ship travel to Khuul and his wife tends to the new lighthouse. The Shipmaster in Khuul now also offers travel service to Ald Velothi.

## World Edits

Replaces the buildings and interiors of Ald Velothi with new exterior cluttering, detailing, and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with most other Ald Velothi overhaul mods. Confirmed compatible with URH - Ald Velothi and Devilish Rocky West Gash.
