The isolated patch of Ashlands to the south now has its own design to distinguish it from the foyadas and the neighboring Molag Amur.

The roughly circular shape of the area, steep walls, with only one path in and out all give it the impression of a secondary caldera to the bigger Red Mountain. Now its outer walls are built up and emphasized to make that impression stronger.

The trees have been swapped for tall petrified ones, the area is a similar latitude to the West Gash and the Bitter Coast so may have at one point in its history been vibrant from the fertile volcanic soil before the blight and the Ashlands crept in and killed it. Now it is just a tall dead forest.

## World Edits:

Lightweight flora and terrain overhaul of the southern Ashlands, adding new assets but making NO landscape edits and NO edits to interiors.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with most other mods. No known conflicts.
