An overhaul of Palansour, and some additional lore added to the place.

## Summary:

The caverns of Palansour is yet another overlooked location in Morrowind. Sure there is a short quest to rescue someone, but it can be very easy to overlook as no vannila quests or guilds send you there.

There is also the case of the smugglers themselves, why are there scamps and an Ogrim there, and why did they simply not just kill everyone in the cave?

This mod will give you that answer and a better dungeon to explore. Using Rocky West Gash as a major part of the cavern interior, making it stand out from other caves in the region.

## World Edits:

Overhauls and expands the vanilla game dungeon of Palansour in the West Gash region. Makes minor exterior additions with a secondary dungeon entrance.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Rocky West Gash and highly compatible with all other mods that don't modify Palansour.
