A modest expansion to Indarys manor, aiming to make it feel a bit more rewarding to the player. The main manor gets an almost complete overhaul, whereas the side buildings get a bit of an overhaul to ownership of items (making the merchants have more stuff to sell), replacing most of the containers with OAAB variants for more variety and a bit of cluttering.

## World Edits:

Primarily an interior overhaul of Indarys manor and associated interiors. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Compatible with all other mods, except other Bal Isra and Indarys overhauls.
