> The person taking care of the Telvanni Temple in Vivec is, oddly enough, a Breton. What kind of person is she? This mod adds a questline to try and answer that question.

## World Edits

Adds quest-related NPCs and objects to two vanilla interior cells. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Comes with a BCOM compatibility version. Otherwise, may have conflicts with mods that overhaul or change the layout of the Telvanni Canton Tribunal Temple.
