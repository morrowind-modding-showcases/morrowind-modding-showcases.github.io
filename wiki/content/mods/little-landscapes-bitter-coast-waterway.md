You know that coastline between Seyda Neen and the shipwreck? This mod overhauls that.

## World Edits:

Exterior clutter and terrain changes to the Bitter Coast region north of Seyda Neen.

Makes landscape edits to the following cells: (-3, -8), (-4, -7), (-4, -8), (-5, -7), and (-5, -8).

Makes NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with the following mods:
- OAAB Shipwrecks
- The Sable Dragon
- The Plague Doctor
- Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees

No Known Conflicts Reported.
