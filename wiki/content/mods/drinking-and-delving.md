> Go on an adventure to obtain a strange relic for an even stranger client. To get started, check your pockets at level 7 or later around Sadrith Mora.

## Features:

- New dungeon to explore
- New tower with potential trader
- NPCs from One Side of the Coin﻿ may appear if that mod is installed and a certain quest outcome has been reached
- Adds another altar that can be used if using Shade of the Revenant altar resource

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Azura's Coast, with a new Telvanni tower and associated exterior edits, and overhauls the vanilla tomb of Verelnim. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with Tombs of Zafirbel Bay but two separate compatibility patches exist, one that moves the tomb edits to [Hlervu Ancestral Tomb](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59867), and one that [makes an entirely new tomb](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/59804). Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods.
