> Uses just the roof and tower pieces from Dallaras Balmora/Suran (with permission) to make a more vanilla friendly version.
>
> The intent is to make Suran have a slightly different architectural identity distinct from Balmora.
>
> Adds a tower to the smith that is available to buy as a small player home, and several small storage spaces above various shops.

## World Edits

Adds new assets to Suran, including one new interior cell. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Comes with an alternative versions for Suran O Suran and White Suran. May have conflicts with mods moving or changing the layout of Suran.
