> A thorough visual overhaul of Seyda Neen as a wave-swept but active fishing village with Imperial presence. BCOMpatible version available.

## Features:

- A lot of shore clutter, both natural and built. Seaweed-choked shoreline with rockpools and plenty of beachcombing to be had!
- A decent overhaul of the town's interiors. The Census and Excise offices have been spruced up, and the warehouse should now feel more like a warehouse and less like a corridor.
- Arrille's Tradehouse has been given a good makeover too, with plenty of new things to browse!
- Terurise Girvayne is now a functional apothecary, providing home-made remedies to her fellow villagers.
- A new house for a new NPC...
  -...which is next to a new house that has been abandoned and can be claimed! No quest involved yet.

## World Edits

Interior and exterior overhaul of the village of Seyda Neen and its environs. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Alternative versions are available for compatibility with Hlaalu Seyda Neen and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind. Confirmed compatible with Shroomda Neen, Vality's Bitter Coast Mod, Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees, and Tales of Seyda Neen. Confirmed conflicts with Immersive Seyda Neen and OAAB Seyda Neen - Damp Little Squat. Will likely have conflicts with other Seyda Neen city overhauls.
