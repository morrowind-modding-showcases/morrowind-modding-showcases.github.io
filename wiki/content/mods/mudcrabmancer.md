> Pelagiad may not be what is seems on the surface. Work with new companions and discover the true crime lords running the town and steal their powers for your own use. This mod includes new mudcrab themed light armor and a robe enchanted with special effects that grant you an army of mudcrab followers.

## World Edits

Adds two new locations outside Pelagiad, along with a handful of quest-related NPCs and objects. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods. No known conflicts.
