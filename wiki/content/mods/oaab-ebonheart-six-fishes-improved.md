> Overhauls the Six Fishes tavern in Ebonheart to be more memorable and lively. 2 new quests. The top floor is now an officers lounge, and a bottom floor with a kitchen and rental rooms has been added.

## Features:

- 5 new NPCs
- 2 new quests, including one that gives you an actual reason to go to Solstheim
- Totally overhauled interior using OAAB/TD assets
- Ground floor has been improved but keeps the same footprint, so most mods changing the Six Fishes (eg Dartboards and Broadsheets & Noticeboards) should be compatible by default
- A new officers lounge on the first floor, where nobles, East Empire Company officials, and off-duty legionnaires can come to hobnob with the best kind of company
- You can now actually see the Six Fishes
- The inn now has a kitchen - aka a way to cook the food they sell!
- The rental room is improved, with more storage for your stuff now. It's also a little more expensive -- Agning knows he has a monopoly on rooms in Ebonheart and is taking full advantage.

## World Edits

Minor exterior additions to Ebonheart, overhauls the interior of the Six Fishes tavern including new assets and NPCs. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, but should be compatible with nearly all other mods.
