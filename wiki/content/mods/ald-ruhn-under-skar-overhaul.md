> Overhaul of the Under-Skar & Council Hall in Ald-ruhn

## Features:

- Moves all Redoran vendors' shops to the Manor District
- Moves all Redoran retainers' houses to the Manor District
- Overhauls the above using assets from TR_Data an OAAB_Data
- Overhauls the Under-Skar
- Overhauls and expands the Redoran Council Hall

## World Edits

Minor exterior cell edits, primarily overhauls the interior cells of Under-Skar. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will conflict with other mods overhauling or editing Under-Skar.
