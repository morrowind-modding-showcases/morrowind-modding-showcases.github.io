Gray North: Ruined Redaynia implements the cut village of Ald Redaynia as a Velothi settlement, Ashlanders who have abandoned their nomadic traditions to settle as a houseless underclass. In addition, the nearby Velothi tower for which the settlement is named has been overhauled, with a number of events expanding on existing content as well as a new unmarked quest to complete.

Released as part of the Gray North project, an effort to overhaul the region of Sheogorad and its environs, giving the island chain its own distinct character while improving its locals. Major locations such as this are planned to be released independently before being packaged together with the revamping of the region's exteriors.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the exterior of Ald Redaynia, including landscape edits, new buildings, statics, and clutter.

Also overhauls the interior of Ald Redaynia.

## Other Notes:

Will be incompatible with other Ald Redaynia mods and some Sheogorad landscape overhauls.

Note, a newer version of this mod exists by the name [Ashfront - Ruined Redaynia](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/57947).
