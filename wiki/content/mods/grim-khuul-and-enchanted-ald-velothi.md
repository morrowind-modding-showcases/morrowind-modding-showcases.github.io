Another overhaul of two tiny villages - Khuul and Ald Velothi

## Features:
- added many new objects - statics, containers, flora, etc.
- added new trees in Ald Velothi (model from Vurt);
- added a lot of lanterns (Khuul - orange, Ald Velothi-blue);
- potted flowers, household items, fishing poles, baskets, seashells, marshmerrows along the shores of the bay;
- several places for the rest of the local population - benches, a campfire, a pot, pillows;
- added a new and absolutely stunning ivy (model from Momo);
- improvement of the Siltstrider port in Khuul;
- change the location of banners in the Khuul;
- two beautiful boats in Ald Velothi;
- the altar of Saint Veloth, hidden in a natural area in Ald Velothi;
- minor changes to the architecture of the stone landscape to give comfort to the settlement;
- hidden backwater of smugglers (boat, bench, canopy, several containers);
- pack guar at the exit of Ald Velothi;
- interiors are not affected;
- path grid fixed;

## World Edits:

Adds exterior clutter and detailing to the villages of Khuul and Ald Velothi and their surroundings. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Likely to be incompatible with other Khuul and Ald Velothi overhauls. Has a very minor conflict with URH - Ald Velothi and URH - Khuul.
