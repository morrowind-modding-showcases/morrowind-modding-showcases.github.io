> Adds a staffing agency to Ebonheart, this uses OpenMW lua to randomly generate servants the player can hire. All servants will charge a monthly fee of 250 - 350 gold depending on their specialties. It spawns a new set of NPCs every 7 days.

## World Edits

Minor exterior edits adding a new entrance to a new interior in Ebonheart. Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with mods changing the layout of Ebonheart.
