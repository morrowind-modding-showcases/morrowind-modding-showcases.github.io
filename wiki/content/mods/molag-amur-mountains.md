Makes the eastern part of Molag Amur significantly rockier and more mountainous. It is now significantly harder to cross without levitation.

## World Edits:

Adds terrain statics to most of the Molag Amur region, primarily along the tops of hills and mountains, avoiding Mount Kand and Uvirith's Grave (though an optional addon also covers Uvirith's Grave).

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with The Ashlanders, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Tall Dungeons, Native Lands, The Mountain of Fear, virtually all Mount Assarnibibi overhauls, and Finding the Erabenimsun Ashalnder Camp.

A compatiblity patch is available for Terror of Tel Amur and OAAB - Winged Twilight (will otherwise conflict).
