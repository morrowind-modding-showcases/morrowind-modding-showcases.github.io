> This mod adds a trader selling a large variety of bows , arrows, projectile weapons and special equipment that should appeal to ''Ranger'' type characters. The shop is conveniently placed close to the Vivec.silt strider port.

## World Edits

Adds a single new shop to the outskirts of Vivec with associated exterior clutter and new interior. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most Vivec city mods, but may have other conflicts due to proximity to Vivec.
