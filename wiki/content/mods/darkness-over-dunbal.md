> *Upon visiting The End of the World tavern in Dagon Fel, a frantic Dunmer begs you to save his village, far to the north on an island in the Sea of Ghosts. You wearily agree, but upon arriving on the island you realize that you are in way over your head. The island has been shrouded by darkness and evil; everything and everyone has been corrupted, perverted. It is up to you, and you alone, to become the Deliverer of Light and restore Meridia's rule.*

## Features:

- Spiritual sequel to the 2020 mod Invasion of Stormwatch, embark on a unique dungeon delving experience as you battle Namira and her occupying forces in the latest war between Namira and Meridia.
- Explore a large island shrouded in eternal darkness and fog to discover secrets and hunt manifestations of Namira in five uniquely crafted dungeons.
- Complete quests to earn rewards that make navigating dungeons less difficult.
- B O S S  B A T T L E S
- The island and the dungeons have been shrouded in complete darkness. Navigate through the darkness by relighting various light sources using flint or by carefully managing your light supply.
- As you fight to restore Meridia's rule over the island, Namira will work harder to make your life harder. The island itself and its difficulty will react to your progress.

## World Edits

Adds a new island north of the Sheogorad with landscape edits and new locations. Adds a single new NPC to an interior cell in Dagon Fel.

## Other Notes

Confirmed compatible with Tamriel Rebuilt, but will likely have some landscape conflicts with other nearby landmass mods.
