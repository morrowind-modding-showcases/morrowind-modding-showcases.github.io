Outcasts of the Zainab tribe teamed up to establish their own tribe, the Salit tribe. However, they are a bit different than regular outcasts: these people were not exiled, or chose to leave because they are murderers, thieves and thugs, but because of a variety of justifiable reasons: some of them disapproved the soft lifestyle and the materialism of the Zainab, and wanted to live according to the old ways of the Ashlanders, closer to nature. Others were misfits and rebels whose more existence didn't fit into the rigid, traditional way of thinking of the tribe. And there were of course those, who just followed their friends and partners into the new tribe.

But this new tribe is still small, and needs to develop: they don't even have an Ashkhan yet. Are you willing to take this honor and burden on your shoulders? If you're a worthy candidate, you might want to give it a shot. (To begin: Find the Salit Camp, to the southwest of Tel Mora, and speak with the Gulakhan of the tribe, once Dagoth Ur is dead. If Dagoth Ur is still alive, the tribe will remain peaceful, but will mostly refuse to communicate with you.)

Features of this mod include:

- An overhaul of the vanilla Salit Camp: Its inhabitants have been pacified, it has been seriously expanded with new yurts and members, and it is more detailed now than in vanilla
- LGNPC-ish dialogue for the members of the tribe, all of them would have something unique to say (mostly through their greetings and through the topic "background")
- The usual services expected in an Ashlander Camp: a trader, a healer and a wise woman
- The opportunity to become an Ashkhan (your new title in the "Ashlanders" faction will be displayed on your character sheet)
- A small player home: an Ashkhan's Yurt
- Guars!

The mod contains seven quests:

- Become the Ashkhan of the Salit tribe
- Become the Nerevarine (as in: chosen champion of an Ashlander tribe) of the Salit tribe﻿ and receive a unique magical artifact, just like in case of the other four Ashlander tribes
- A radiant quest: turn in wickwheat in exchange for healing potions
- Appease the Zainab and pay a tribute to them in exchange for peace
- Put your new Ashlander Camp on a trade route to ensure the prosperity of your tribe
- Start a guar herd to diversify the ways the tribe can sustain itself
- A personal quest: Acquire the book, The Hope of the Redoran, to a bookworm member of the tribe and have it signed by both Athyn Sarethi and Shardie to make him happy

Compatibility:

The mod is compatible with Ashlander Quests and Main Quest Overhaul.
