> True Library of Vivec is a mod which adds new clutter and a few minor details in order to make the Library of Vivec feel more like an actual library.

## World Edits

Interior only overhaul of the Library of Vivec. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with all other mods, minus other Library of Vivec overhauls.
