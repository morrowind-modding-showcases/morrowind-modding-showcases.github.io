This little replacement make the old dunmer's strongholds less empty and a little bit more dangerous

## World Edits:

Primarily modifies the exterior cells of the Dunmer strongholds of Vvardenfell, including modest landscape edits, clutter placement, and new NPC enemies.

Technically marked as modifying the stronghold interiors, doesn't actually make any changes to them.

## Other Notes:

Mod comes with a modular and all-in-one package option. Players can pick and choose which strongholds they want to include in their game.

Will conflict with other stronghold mods like The Northern Strongholds, Berandas Overhaul, Marandus Rebuilt, Kogoruhn Expanded, and Andasreth Stronghold Overhaul.

But due to the modular options, players can avoid conflicts if they so choose.
