> A lush and spacious hideout in Vivec, Telvanni Underworks, with plenty of atmosphere and plantlife. Clear the home of the Daedric inhabitants and find out its story.

## World Edits

Adds a new location to the Telvanni Underworks in Vivec, but otherwise makes NO exterior cell edits and NO landscaping edits.

## Other Notes

This is a modified version of [Vivec Abode by Ravensong](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/329), and consequently, cannot be used with that mod. Otherwise, should be compatible with most other mods.
