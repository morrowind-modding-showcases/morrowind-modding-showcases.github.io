Overhauls the Strongholds of Valenvaryon, Falasmaryon, Indoranyon, Falensaro, Andraseth, Rotheran, and Telsaro.

## World Edits:

Adds new clutter, detailing, and dungeon expansions to the exteriors of a number of Dunmer strongholds, as well as interior overhauls and new dungeon interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Comes with an all-in-one package and modular options for players to pick and choose which stronghold overhauls they want.

Will conflict with Better Dunmer Strongholds and Andraseth Expanded, but due to the modular options, is otherwise fairly compatible with most other mods.

Includes a compatibility patch for Sheogorad - Groves of the North and is confirmed compatible with Spines of Madness. Will conflict with Ashfront - Sheogorad.
