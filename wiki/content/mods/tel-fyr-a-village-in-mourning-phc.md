A Village in Mourning adds a small village on the grounds of Lord Fyr, complete with basic services and a unique atmosphere.

This small hamlet was first founded by the relatives of those lost to the Divine Disease.
Wanting to be close to their loved ones, desperate people started setting up households just outside of Lord Fyr's tel.
As the village grew the denizens became paranoid of living so close to the Corprusarium resulting in them wearing enchanted masks to try and avoid getting sick themselves.

The town will serve the player character as a place to stock up before heading into the grotesque bowels of the Corprusarium.

## Features:

- Basic services such as a Tradehouse, Alchemist, and Armorer.
- A new dock with Travel Service to and from Tel Aruhn
- A revamped and unique atmosphere and aesthetic thanks to OAAB_Data and Tamriel_Data
- New Disease resistant masks for sale at the Tradehouse
- Designed to be used in conjunction with other Tel fyr and Corprusarium mods.
- The Tower itself and immediate surroundings have been left unchanged.

## World Edits:

Primarily adds new structures, statics and clutter to the exterior of Tel Fyr.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, Sorrow Heart of Tel Fyr, and Azura's Coast Redone.

A [compatibility patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/57939) is available for Shroomdoliers and a Fisherooman and Marbled Zafirbel Bay.
