> 4 Regions, 4 Themes, 4 Sea Forts! Above the wave's upon the horizon sits a Sea Fortress, these act as mini dungeons for plundering, which sit a good swim away. Don't forget to use those ladders!
>
> Acting as mini dungeons, scale these 4 leveled forts and fight the long forgotten that Dwell within! make your way to the top floor, kill the boss, get the key, head down to the basement level and unlock the door to your new found wealth. As an optional objective they'll be a second random key hidden somewhere on either one of the floors to unlock an addition door on the top floor storage room for even more riches..

## World Edits

Adds new exterior location to the waters off the coasts of the West Gash, Bitter Coast, Sheogorad, and Azura's Coast. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes

Should be highly compatible with most other mods, new locations are put in relatively low-use coastal areas. No major conflicts currently reported.
