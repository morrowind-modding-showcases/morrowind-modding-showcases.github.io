Another replacement of a beautiful cute sleepy Caldera.

## Features:
- added a variety of household items;
- added benches, containers, carts, flags, lanterns;
- added a lot of flower pots, wild flora and trees (using the model from Vurt);
- added a pack guar at the entrance to the town;
- added elements of stone walls to create a cozy atmosphere;
- added two wickets;
- redesigned lighting;
- added a blacksmith shop on the street in front of the armory entrance;
- added ivy to the walls;
- added a small vegetable garden;
- added the sanctuary of St. Felms in the backyard, in the depths of the settlement;
- path grid fixed.

## World Edits:

Primarily an exterior clutter overhaul of Caldera, adding new detailing and decorations to the buildings and streets of the town.

makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with other Caldera city overhaul mods, in addition to Caldera Governors Manor Redone (floating lanterns and flags), but will otherwise be compatible with most other nearby Caldera mods.
