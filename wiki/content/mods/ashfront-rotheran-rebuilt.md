Overhauls the Dunmer stronghold of Rotheran, expanding on existing themes and implementing a scripted encounter.

Released as part of the Ashfront project, an effort to diversify the environs of Vvardenfell in a vanilla-friendly fashion through expansion of existing themes, implementation of prerelease concepts, improved detailing, and addition of points of interest.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of the Dunmer stronghold of Rotheran.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with other Rotheran overhauls like The Northern Strongholds and Better Dunmer Strongholds.

Requires Ashfront Sheogorad.
