> The 3.1 version of Hoghead the Horrible's classic era city extension mod Vivec Expansion, adding new detail, clutter, and locations around the city of Vivec, with new slums cluttering the canals.

## World Edits

Extensive exterior edits to the city of Vivec, adding new structures, details, NPCs, and clutter, along with landscape edits and new interiors.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with any mods that move or other extend the cantons of Vivec, but otherwise should be compatible with mods that simply overhaul the cantons themselves. Confirmed compatible with Dramatic Vivec, and confirmed conflict with Vivec Docks.

A merged version of Vivec Expansion with Redesigned Vivec [can be found here](https://web.archive.org/web/20210613203042/https://mw.modhistory.com/download--14742). Note, this is based on an older version of Vivec Expansion, and may have bugs that are fixed in version 3.1.
