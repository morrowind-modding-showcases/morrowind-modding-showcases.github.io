“Plumb the veiled hollows that slumber beneath the eldest stones of Tamriel.
Long shunned by memory and unmarked by any chronicle, the deep-forgotten vaults of Ald Redaynia now stir, awaiting the footfall of one bold enough to trespass their silence.”

## Features:
- It’s an average sized adventure with lots of locations, hazards, artifacts,enemies,etc... 
- 16 locations in total, both large and small.
- Recommend(optional) updating the settings"Distand Land"for this mode(to see more distant corners).
- There will be no Journal entries! The task is simple - to destroy three powerful "Lich", each of whom has portal keys.
- Having gathered all the keys (respectively killing three Liches), you will be able to pass through three portals that lead to a place that has been hidden from sight for many centuries.(basically, just like the previous ones)))

## World Edits:

Makes a number of minor edits to exterior cells, including landscape edits, in addition to modifications to a number of vanilla interiors.
