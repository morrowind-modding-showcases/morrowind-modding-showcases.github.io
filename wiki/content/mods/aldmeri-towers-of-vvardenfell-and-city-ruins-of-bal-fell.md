Adds ruined Aldmeri Towers to Ald Redaynia, Bal Fell, Tel Aruhn, and Tel Mora. Also adds ruined Velothi buildings around Bal Fell.

## World Edits:

Minor static additions to a number of exterior cells in the form of new buildings.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Should be largely compatible with other mods, but may run into conflicts with extensive landscape overhauls, particularly in the Sheogorad with Ald Redaynia.
