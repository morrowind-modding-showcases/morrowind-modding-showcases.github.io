This mod places saplings from OAAB_Data in most regions on Vvardenfell.

## World Edits:

Flora overhaul placing new flora assets across most regions of Vvardenfell, including the Grazelands, Ascadian Isles, Bitter Coast, West Gash, Azura's Coast, and Sheogorad.

Makes NO landscape edits and NO interior cell edits.

## Other Notes:

Comes with a wide array of compatibility patches and should, in theory, be largely compatible with most other mods that don't heavily modify the terrain of vanilla regions.
