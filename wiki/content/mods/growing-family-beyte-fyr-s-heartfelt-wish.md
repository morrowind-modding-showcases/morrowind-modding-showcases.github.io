> In this mod you get to help Beyte Fyr make a child, in a very Fyr way. To start the quest, talk to her in Tel Fyr.
>
> While your character doesn't need to use magic for this mod, it definitely helps. I also advice a slightly higher level, as it will help you get more out of the locations the quest sends you to.

## World Edits

Interior cell edits to a small number of locations. Includes an alternative version with no edits to Massama Cave. Makes NO landscape or exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods.
