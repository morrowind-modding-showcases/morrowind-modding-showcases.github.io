> A Player Home is added to the Lower Waistworks of Vivec's Foreign Quarter. The home is called "Goran Manor", named after the previous owner, "Theo Goran". A Note from him is left on a Level 50 locked door and, when unlocked, the home becomes the player's home.

## World Edits

Adds an entrance to a new interior cell to the waistworks of the Foreign Quarter Canton. Makes NO landscape edits and NO exterior cell edits.

## Other Notes

Should be compatible with most other mods that don't change the layout of the Foreign Quarter Lower Waistworks.
