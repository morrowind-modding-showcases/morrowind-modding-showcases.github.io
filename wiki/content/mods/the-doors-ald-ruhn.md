> This mod adds some city doors to the city of Ald-ruhn and complete city walls around it. These doors are closed during the night and open in the morning. Wall is slightly inclined according to the terrain, so the it seems more continuous.

## World Edits

Adds new exterior fortifications around the city of Ald'ruhn. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to vanilla locations.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with other Ald'ruhn city overhauls, particularly those that add their own takes to the city's fortifications.
