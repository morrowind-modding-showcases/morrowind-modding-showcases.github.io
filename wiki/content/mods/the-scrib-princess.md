A quest about kwama, talking animals, a curse, and possibly an old friend.

Up in the northern Grazelands, the Ahemmusa Ashlanders tell the story of a princess who was lost to the wastes. But just who is this princess? That's for you to discover!

This mod contains one main quest to find this mysterious princess, as well as two side quests that you will have to discover yourself. There is also an overhaul for the Maelu Egg Mine, and the Ravel Ancestral Tomb.

## World Edits:

Overhauls and details two dungeon interiors in the Grazelands, along with a few exterior edits.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, will only conflict with mods that also overhaul the Maelu Egg Mine and Ravel Ancestral Tomb. Otherwise should be compatible with all other mods in the Grazelands.
