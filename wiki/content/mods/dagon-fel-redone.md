Completely replaces the assets for Dagon Fel. The Dunmer shacks are now TR Nord ones with interiors to match, the dock was replaced with its Nord set equivalent, furniture was swapped were appropriate, The Arrow has been replaced with a Nordic ship with matching interior, the watchtower is now stone, a windmill was added, and the inn/tavern was retextured and given a new interior following the footprint of the old one.

## World Edits:

Overhaul of both the exteriors and interiors of Dagon Fel, replacing buildings and adding new clutter and details.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Confirmed compatible with Dagon Fel Lighthouse, but will conflict with most other Dagon Fel overhaul mods.
