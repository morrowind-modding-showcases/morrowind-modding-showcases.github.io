Vivec always had the problem of not being an actual city. Other than patrons and shopkeepers, there were hardly any people actually living in Vivec. and where would they? Vivec was crammed and claustrophobic, although it looked huge from the outside. This mod aims to fix that.

## World Edits:

Extensive overhaul of Vivec and its surroundings, including virtually all interior cells.

## Other Notes:

Will conflict with most other Vivec city overhauls, but contains a number of compatibility patches, including for BCOM, Welcome to the Arena, OAAB - Tombs and Towers, and more besides.
