> From land to sea exploration, Help Pendragon find Captain Flameheart! and stop the ritual. and his return once and for all!

## Features:

- A few interiors with custom cave's.
- New meshes and textures.
- Jump puzzles.
- Scripted stuff.
- New skeleton type.
- Custom boss.
- Some voiced lines.
- Comes with a medium sized volcanic island (Far. Far. Far North).
- A Few things added. underwater along the shore line to loot.
- Loot, loot, loot and boss rewards.

## World Edits

Adds a new landmass with associated new locations to the northern Sea of Ghosts, north of the Sheogorad. Also adds a new boat to the harbor of Khuul.

## Other Notes

Compatible with Baal Duun, but may have other landmass conflicts.
