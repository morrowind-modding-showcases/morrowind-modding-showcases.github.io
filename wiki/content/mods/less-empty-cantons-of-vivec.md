> Lightweight clutter mod that uses planters to fill some empty spaces in Vivec

## World Edits

Lightweight exterior detailing to the Vivec cantons, makes NO exterior edits and NO edits to vanilla interiors. Includes optional addons for extra clutter and detailing.

## Other Notes

Compatible with some other Vivec city overhauls, but variable conflicts to be expected, particularly with mods that move the cantons. Note, this mod has already been merged into Beautiful Cities of Morrowind.
