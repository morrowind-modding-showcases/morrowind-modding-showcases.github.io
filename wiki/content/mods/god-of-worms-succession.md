## Introduction

Have you ever wondered where Mannimarco was between the events of The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall and The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion? How Mannimarco returned in Oblivion after achieving godhood during the Warp in the West? Or what the story is behind Mannimarco's second-in-command in Oblivion - Bolor Savel? This mod seeks to provide an answer to these questions through a series of thrilling dungeon crawler-style quests.

## Features

- Five new quests.
- Two new sets of robes.
- Several exciting boss fights.
- An overhauled Savel Ancestral Tomb.
- An opportunity to challenge Mannimarco - The King of Worms.
- A story that bridges two games together and fits in perfectly with vanilla.
- Several new artifacts - A Concept-art Daedric Helmet, the Spelldrinker Amulet, and the Staff of Worms.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the Savel Ancestral Tomb, and adds new quest items and NPCs to a variety of other locations.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Should be highly compatible with other mods, and is confirmed to be compatible with Daedric Shrine Overhaul FULL.

Technically an updated version of King of Worms.

This mod is also merged into [Morrowind Extended Cut](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/55615).
