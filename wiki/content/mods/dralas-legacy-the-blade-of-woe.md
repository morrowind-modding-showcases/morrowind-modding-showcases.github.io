A quest mod and tomb overhaul that uncovers the fate of a Dark Brotherhood artifact: the Blade of Woe.

## Features:

- A multi-part quest
- Dialogue with NPCs and documents that expand on the Dark Brotherhood and Morag Tong history
- An overhaul of the Dralas Ancestral Tomb
- The Blade of Woe (short blade)

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interior of the Dralas Ancestral Tomb, keeping largely the same layout, but with additional clutter.

Adds a single NPC outside of Fort Moonmoth.

## Other Notes:

Highly compatible with other mods, currently no known conflicts.
