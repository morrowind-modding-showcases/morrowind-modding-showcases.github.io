An overhaul of Vassamsi grotto, and the island it is located on.

## Description:

This mod adds a new dungeon to the island that Vassamsi Grotto is located on, which connects to both it and the Ancestral tomb nearby. As such, this mod also improves both the two dungeons in question.

The landscape of the island has also been upgraded and detailed so that it's no longer just an empty rock with next to nothing on it. A Telvanni style entrance to the mine has been added, as well as a ruined exterior structure commonly found outside Kogoruhn.

## World Edits:

Modifies the interiors and exteriors of Vassamsi Grotto and Baram Ancestral Tomb, including new exterior terrain assets.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

This mod has been merged into Marbled Zafirbel Bay, and will conflict with that mod.

Likewise, will conflict with any other dungeon overhauls of the Vassamsi Grotto and Baram Ancestral Tomb.

Will be compatible with most other mods in the Azura's Coast region.
