> This mod replaces all the 'leafy' Bitter Coast trees with my own "speedtree" like trees, a similar technique to what was used in Oblivion. The plugin adds many more trees to the Bitter Coast to give the region a more dense atmosphere.

## World Edits

Landscape overhaul of a large chunk of the Bitter Coast region, including new tree and flora assets and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

A compatibility patch is included in [Graht Morrowind Swamp Trees](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/49771), and a full compatibility version providing support for Shipyards of Vvardenfell, OAAB Seyda Neen, and Beautiful Cities of Morrowind [can be found here.](https://www.nexusmods.com/morrowind/mods/53548)

Otherwise will have variable compatibility with mods set in the Bitter Coast.
