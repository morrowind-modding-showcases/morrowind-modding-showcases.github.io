Complete remake of House Hlaalu's Stronghold with new exterior/interiors, gameplay mechanics, and quests.

## Features:

- Complete remake of Rethan Manor featuring new gameplay mechanics and an income system
- Your business ledger introduces an upgrade system for the Manor, allowing you to add new rooms and increase your Stronghold's income through the acquisition of different assets
- A new lore-friendly questline that serves as an epilogue to the vanilla House Hlaalu questline. Your actions during both the vanilla questline and the new questline will have consequences.
- Connect with your retainers, complete quests to recruit them to your stronghold. Complete personal quests to aid in their growth.
- Current quest count: 14. More are planned for future updates.

## World Edits:

Overhauls the interiors and exteriors of Rethan Manor, including landscape edits. Also includes new additions to a number of other locations and interiors with quest objects and NPCs.

## Other Notes:

Compatibility patches are available for Odai - Lifeblood of Balmora and Balmora - The City of Contrasts.

Otherwise, confirmed compatible with the following mods:
- God of Worms
- Roleplay Options for Great House Hlaalu
- Kilcunda's Balmora
- OAAB Ashen Divide
- BCOM (base version)
- Concept Art Plantations

Confirmed Conflicts with Amazing Balmora Expansion, Quorn's Resource Integration and Roaring Arena
