> Chargen mod. Start the game in your house to the south of Ebonheart, next to the lake.

## Features:

- A new player home
- A brand new chargen experience: Start the game in your home to the south of Ebonheart
- A new way to start the Main Quest: A courier will deliver the letter and the package to you to your home
- A short warm-up quest before that: Take care of a rat which took up residence in your garden, among your beautiful flowers
- A new item, (as a reward for the warm-up quest) which might be useful for a low-level character

## World Edits

Adds a new building south of Ebonheart with associated new interior and landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Will likely conflict with other mods set south of Ebonheart, particularly with landscape conflicts, but has been confirmed compatible with Ebonheart outskirt 'n' Lighthouse district and Vampire Realism.
