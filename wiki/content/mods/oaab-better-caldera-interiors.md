Improves most interiors in Caldera in a lore-friendly way using OAAB/TD assets.

Features:
- 18 cells have been edited to bring them up to date with modern interior standards
- NPC personalities, occupations, factions, and class differences are emphasised in a way that hopefully helps support Morrowind's worldbuilding

Caldera Mages Guild, Ghorak Manor, Shenk's Shovel, the Governor's Mansion and Caldera Mine itself are not edited

Confirmed Compatible with: Caldera (N'wahs and Mushrooms Team), Cozy Caldera, Mystery of Sara Shenk, Expedition to Mzelthuand, Caldera Mines Expanded, and Izi-Inspired Caldera.

Conflicts with Beautiful Cities of Morrowind (unless using a tes3cmd patch).
