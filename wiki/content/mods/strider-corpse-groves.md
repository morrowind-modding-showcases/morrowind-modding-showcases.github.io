The silt strider corpses of the northwestern Ashlands are now food for fungi. Requires Tamriel_Data.

## World Edits:

Landscape and clutter overhaul of the central northern Ashlands, adding new flora and terrain assets, as well as landscape edits and texturing.

Makes NO edits to vanilla interiors.

## Other Notes:

Compatibility patches are available for Strider Burial and Dwemer Legacy Reforged - Arkngthunch-Sturdumz﻿.

Should be compatible with the majority of other northern Ashlands mods.
