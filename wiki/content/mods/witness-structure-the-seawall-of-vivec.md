A city expansion for Vivec, Witness Structure adds the Seawall of Vivec across the southern end of Norvayn Bay. Complete with new quests, hundreds of lines of unique dialogue, new services, secrets to discover, and more.

## Features:

- New lore surrounding the Seawall, its history, and its religious significance
- A fully redesigned Seawall exterior, with a more unique and aesthetic layout
- Functional sea gates with new models courtesy of Vidi Aquam
- 30+ richly detailed interior cells to explore
- 7 new side and faction quests related to the Seawall
- Nearly 300 new dialogue entries
﻿- Dozens of new lore books and flavor text documents
- Treasures and many secrets to uncover

## World Edits:

Adds new structures along the entrance of the Norvayn Bay, with new landscape cluttering and detailing and associated interiors.

Makes NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes:

Comes with a patch for Little Landscapes - Vivec Islands. Confirmed conflict with Mournhold Style Canton.

Otherwise, compatible with all Vivec city overhauls and expansions, Ascadia - Land of Pilgrimage, OAAB Shipwrecks, Vivec Lighthouse, and nearly all other mods.
