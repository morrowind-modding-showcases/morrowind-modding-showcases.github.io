## World Edits:

Edits five exterior cells in and around Tel Branora, primarily adding new static objects.

Note: NO Landscape Edits

## Other Notes:

Confirmed Compatible with Tel Branora Expansion, Beautiful Cities of Morrowind, RR Mod Series - Tel Branora Lighthouse, and Azuras Coast Redone.
