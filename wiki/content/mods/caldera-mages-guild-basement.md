> Adds a basement to the Caldera Mages Guild decorated with OAAB and some custom assets. Two new mages have goods to sell, new Colovian hats as well as hoods from the OAAB data set. Another mage has new alteration scrolls and spells to buy and some from OAAB as well.

## World Edits

Adds a basement to the Caldera Guild of Mages. Makes NO landscape edits and NO edits to exterior cells.

## Other Notes

May have conflicts with mods changing the layout of the Caldera Mages Guild, but confirmed compatible with Caldera Mages Guild Expanded.
