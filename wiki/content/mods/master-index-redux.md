> The wizard Folms Mirel in Caldera is trying to unravel the secrets of the ancient Dunmer Propylon network on Vvardenfel. However, he is missing the indices and needs you to find them.

## Features:

This mod changes several aspects of the Master Index quest and plugin, including

- Edits to dialogue and quest entries.
- The functionality of the Master Index has changed, now involving the use of a new propylon in the Caldera mages guildhall.
- Some quest aspects have changed, including the acquisition of certain propylon indices.
- Some alterations to the Caldera mages guildhall, including a basement workshop for Folms and his custom Propylon.

## World Edits

Edits two interior cells in Caldera, and otherwise makes NO exterior cell edits and NO landscape edits.

## Other Notes

Conflicts with BCOM, but a compatibility patch is available. confirmed compatible with Caldera Mages Guild Expansion. Will likely conflict with other Master Index mods.
